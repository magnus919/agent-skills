# Solo startup research brief

Research question: What enables a business with one accountable founder to work,
and which distinctive constraints warrant specialized guidance? Moderate industry
analysis, completed 2026-11-02. Destination: this tracked skill's references;
source-level extraction is in [source records](source-records.md), with method and
exclusions in [research log](research-log.md).

## Findings and confidence

1. **Define the unit before counting it — high confidence.** Census's nonemployer
category, Gumroad's solo founder with employees, and Storemapper's small-team
transition describe different things. Do not use them interchangeably to estimate
solo-startup prevalence or profit. See census, gumroad, storemapper and team records.
2. **A reachable buyer and bounded promise matter more than cheap production —
moderate confidence.** Buffer's conversations and Dunn's offer design provide
practitioner mechanisms, not a causal estimate. Services and products impose
different delivery and acquisition burdens. See buffer and service records.
3. **Support and absence are design inputs — moderate confidence.** Plausible's
two-founder account exposes the conflict between distribution, support and time
off. Storemapper's growth into a team contradicts a permanently-alone reading.
A solo capacity budget is a design inference, not a universal empirical formula.
See support and team records.
4. **The success criterion must fit the financing and founder — moderate
confidence.** Gumroad illustrates a painful mismatch between venture expectations
and a sustainable smaller operation. Base44 is a real exceptional transaction
involving a team, not a normal solo-founder earnings forecast. See gumroad/wix.
5. **Measure accepted outcomes, total cost and founder time — high confidence as
a measurement recommendation; uncertain magnitude of benefit.** METR's dated
trial and later limitations warn against fixed AI productivity multipliers.
SBA arithmetic must be combined with cash and capacity checks. See metr2025,
metr2026 and sba records.

## Competing explanations and limitations

Lower production cost can enable a founder to test more ideas. It can also make
competition easier while shifting work into distribution, review and support.
Both can coexist. The sources do not isolate AI's causal effect on solo-firm
survival, nor provide a representative denominator of attempts. Founder accounts
are selected, often retrospective and promotional. Plausible is explicitly a
two-person analogy; Buffer and Gumroad are not permanently solo businesses.
No universal failure rate, income target, agent multiplier, or hiring threshold
is justified. Business categories include services, products, creator businesses
and commerce, but direct evidence is strongest for software and adjacent services;
commerce guidance is a transfer hypothesis requiring field validation.

The repository already contains five fictional solo-operator walkthroughs in
`docs/evidence/solo-operator-synthetic-2026-11-01/`. They expose useful arithmetic
and handoff errors but explicitly demonstrate no incremental decision benefit
over competent routing. This skill is a requested focused synthesis, not evidence
that a new bundle or autonomous executive layer is needed.

## Design decisions

Use one short operating-envelope-to-decision procedure. Route interviews, models,
distribution, growth and operating cadence to real existing skills. Retain the
cross-boundary constraints: promise changes invalidate prior economics; one person
must budget sales, delivery, review and support together; profitable units may
still miss owner income/capacity; obligations continue during absence. Treat
outsourcing and hiring as options rather than failures of solo identity.

## Open questions and next evidence

- Does this skill improve real decisions versus route-only assistance? Requires
  consented real-user comparisons; synthetic examples cannot settle it.
- Which support and acquisition loads hold for particular non-software models?
- How much do current AI tools improve accepted throughput in the actual workflow?
- Can customers be served through a founder absence with the proposed fallback?

Preservation gate: all retained sources have dated extraction and limitations;
all material claims map to source IDs; exclusions and inaccessible retrievals are
logged. Behavioral effectiveness and independent review remain unestablished.
