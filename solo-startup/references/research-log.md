## Source evaluation and exclusions

Every retained source, material extraction, confidence limit and use appears in
[source records](source-records.md). Primary documents establish what their authors
reported; self-reported business results are not independently audited. Formal
method limitations outrank headlines. Selection used relevance, authority,
traceability and known conflicts rather than a fabricated numeric credibility score.

Rejected Groktocrawl discovery hits: groundworkblog.com/articles/micro-saas-customer-support-solo-founder;

These surfaced vendor claims and unattributed thresholds; not followed as evidence.
Failory aggregate failure percentages, social reposts, news summaries and generic
shutdown lists were rejected as denominator-mismatched or weaker than primary
accounts. No 90% failure statistic was adopted. The motivating YouTube video
was a discovery prompt only; its transcript was not verified in this investigation
and no findings depend on its claims.

## Citation chains and stopping decision

METR 2025 → explicit 2026 follow-up; Tringas ebook → chapter attempt (failed) and
independently read team update. Source selection deliberately sought retrenchment,
team transitions, support costs and study limitations to challenge exceptional
success stories. Stop at mechanism coverage for this bounded design task, not at
a claim of comprehensive industry prevalence. Shutdowns and non-software models
remain weakly covered; profitable survivors do not establish survival probability.
