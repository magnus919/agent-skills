# Solo Startup

Build a business that fits one founder's time, cash, and responsibilities.

## Why Install This Skill

Cheap tools make it easier to produce things. They do not automatically find
customers, collect cash, answer support, or cover a founder's time away. This skill
helps turn an idea into a bounded offer and an honest operating decision.

It works for consulting, productized services, software, digital products, and
other owner-operated businesses. It connects customer evidence, distribution,
economics and delivery capacity while using existing specialist skills for depth.
It supports staying small as well as deliberately growing beyond one person.

## Quick Start

Try: “I have 12 hours a week and want to test a reporting service. Help me choose
a promise, price hypothesis, and first paid pilot without overcommitting.”

## Triggers

- Choosing a solo business or validating a first offer
- Comparing a service with a product under limited time and cash
- Reconciling sales, support and delivery in a one-person business
- Deciding whether AI, contractors, or hiring actually relieve a bottleneck

## Requirements

No runtime or credentials required. Fresh external claims need research access;
specialist skills are useful for interviews, financial models and distribution.
The research is a bounded evidence review, not a success-rate estimate or a
promise of founder income. Examples are synthetic, not customer results.
