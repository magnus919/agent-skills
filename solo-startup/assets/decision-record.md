# Solo business decision record

- Date, decision and current stage:
- Founder goal: owner income, available hours, autonomy/growth preferences:
- Cash/learning loss cap, runway, existing obligations and unknowns:
- Model and dated customer promise (scope, price, exclusions, support terms):
- Buyer/problem/workaround and reachable acquisition route:
- Evidence: source/date, commitment conditions, delivered outcome, repeat use:
- Contradictory evidence and remaining uncertainty:
- Economics: price, all known variable costs, contribution, fixed costs,
  founder labor valuation boundary, collections/refunds, owner income target:
- Capacity: total hours less sales/admin/support/maintenance/reserve; delivery
  hours per unit; affordable unit capacity versus required units:
- Stress cases: delayed payment, support surge, concentration loss, absence:
- Primary bottleneck; specialist handoff and result required to return:
- Automation/contractor option: net time/cost, review owner, fallback:
- Next test: hypothesis, method, budget/hours cap, date, observable success:
- Proceed / revise / stop criteria; next review date:

Keep unknowns explicit. Record versions when scope, price or support promises
change. A proposal is not a customer commitment or an executed experiment.
