# Portfolio measurement contract

- Business decision, owner, pilot scope/budget and exclusions:
- Registry: stable site/tenant/customer IDs, domain/hostname/path, property scope/overlaps, cohort, niche/intent, geography/language, offer/partner/template version, effective dates:
- Stage definitions: search visibility / measured visits / handoffs / partner outcomes / collected revenue:
- Per stage: source, grain, denominator, time zone, currency, freshness, revision window, retention, coverage and owner:
- Query aggregation and overlap policy before joining sources:
- Data state vocabulary: zero / unknown / no integration / privacy suppressed / collection failure / pending revision:
- Partner protocol: authorized destination, opaque ID, authentication, event schema, idempotency, timestamp semantics, statuses, refunds/corrections, attribution window and reconciliation:
- Consent/purpose/jurisdiction review; tenant access and minimization:
- Source quotas, expected partitions, backoff, completeness caveats and cost budget:
- Dashboard contract: weighted totals, distributions, comparable cohorts, coverage/freshness and drilldown dimensions:
- Acceptance fixtures: overlaps, anonymized buckets, duplicates, refunds, timezone shifts, delayed export, absent partners, consent refusal, 1,001-site registry:
- Synthetic test destinations and change approval/rollback:
- Pilot result, uncertainty, expand/repair/stop decision and next review:
