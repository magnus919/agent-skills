# Managing Editor

Keep editorial work moving from first request through final release, with clear owners, reliable handoffs, and standards that survive deadline pressure.

## Why Install This Skill

Editorial work crosses writers, editors, researchers, reviewers, designers, subject experts, legal or communications teams, and publishing systems. Missed handoffs and unclear decision rights can lead to late work, avoidable rework, weak evidence, or an uncorrected public error. This skill gives an agent a practical method for coordinating the whole operation while making the local roles and approval rules explicit.

It works across newsrooms, magazines, books, scholarly journals, digital content, and nonprofit publications. It includes guidance for assigning bounded work to contributors or subagents, so a team can delegate research, drafting, challenge review, copyediting, and production checks while keeping one accountable editor in control of publication decisions.

## Quick Start

Load the skill and give the agent the publication type, audience, goal, deadline and time zone, known contributors, and who can approve editorial direction or publication. For a new assignment, start with [`templates/editorial-plan.md`](templates/editorial-plan.md) and create a brief for each contributor. For delegated work, use [`templates/delegation-packet.md`](templates/delegation-packet.md).

## Triggers

- Plan or coordinate a publication, issue, book, newsroom shift, campaign, or editorial calendar.
- Commission and brief writers, manage review deadlines, unblock handoffs, or resolve competing editorial requests.
- Review a draft through fact verification, copyediting, approvals, production, and release.
- Delegate research, drafting, challenge review, or production checks to contributors or subagents.
- Establish correction, escalation, or publication-readiness processes.

## Requirements

No runtime dependency. Use the relevant organization, publication, legal, privacy, accessibility, ethics, style, and release policies. The templates are Markdown or CSV files and need no special software.
