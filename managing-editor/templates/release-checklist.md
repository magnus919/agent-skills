# Release checklist

Adapt this list to the publication's release policy and subject matter. Checking a box means the named owner inspected the evidence, not that a tool merely generated a completion message.

## Editorial and factual

- [ ] Approved brief and current master are identified.
- [ ] The work answers its assignment and stays within approved scope.
- [ ] Material factual claims, quotes, names, dates, statistics, and links are verified or explicitly qualified.
- [ ] Conflicting evidence and important omissions have been considered.
- [ ] Required source, author, reviewer, and contributor credits are correct.

## Standards, rights, and approvals

- [ ] Applicable editorial, scientific, organizational, and house-style rules are followed.
- [ ] Required legal, privacy, safety, subject-expert, brand, and executive approvals are recorded.
- [ ] Permissions, licenses, releases, image rights, and confidential-data handling are cleared.
- [ ] Conflicts, embargoes, peer-review blinding, and sensitive sources are protected.
- [ ] Accessibility needs and alternate formats are addressed.

## Production and release

- [ ] Headline, deck, captions, byline, metadata, citations, figures, tables, alt text, and links match the approved master.
- [ ] Rendered web, print, email, or other output has been inspected in its final form.
- [ ] Required proofing and specialist checks are complete.
- [ ] Named release owner authorized publication.
- [ ] Version, date/time, location, and approval record are stored appropriately.

## Follow-up

- [ ] Monitoring or feedback owner is named where needed.
- [ ] Correction, update, withdrawal, and escalation path is known.
- [ ] Remaining risks or deferred tasks have owners and dates.
