# SRE operational evidence for governance

SRE submits evidence and recommendations; accountable humans supported by AI governance determine thresholds and authority. This packet is not approval.

- Capability/environment/action class, current grant/revision/expiry, policy ceiling and requested decision:
- Observation dates, workload/incident severity mix, representative coverage and excluded situations:
- Action opportunities/attempts/completions/unknown effects; numerator and denominator for every rate:
- Customer journey/SLO impact, durable-state correctness, dependency recovery and agent-free observation results:
- Harm and near misses: unsafe mutations, false closure, missed/escalation failures, data exposure/loss, excess paging:
- Human review minutes, interrupted reviews, rework, on-call/alert burden and staffing capacity:
- Time to diagnose/mitigate/verify, compute/tool cost and total cost including human review; baseline comparison:
- Freshness, missing telemetry, version/drift changes and unresolved causal uncertainty:
- Held-out replay/fault/no-op results and independent grader provenance; failures and untested paths:
- External enforcement receipts: scope, expiry/revocation, conflicting policy, alternate/child paths and controller outage:
- Rollback/compensation execution evidence; concurrency and ambiguous-effect reconciliation evidence:
- Incident-learning follow-ups: implemented change, verification, observed effect and remaining gaps:
- Proposed maintain/expand/reduce/suspend/revoke decision and evidence limitations; human decision reference when available:

Separate documented, configured, tested and observed-in-production controls. Do not report benchmark success or declarative eval validity as deployed safety proof.
