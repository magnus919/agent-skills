## Agreed actions

| Action / scope | Accepted owner or unconfirmed | Agreed timing or unconfirmed | Agreement evidence | Status/dependency evidence and date |

## Proposals and unresolved questions
Clearly distinguish proposals from commitments. Preserve consequences and unknowns.

## Review and later corrections
Identify corrections versus later decisions. Do not append private organizer suggestions.

When follow-through is requested, preserve later changes and status evidence;
completed work is distinct from proof that the intended outcome improved.
