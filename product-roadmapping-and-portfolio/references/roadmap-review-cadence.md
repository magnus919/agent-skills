# Roadmap Review Cadence

Design and run roadmap reviews producing decisions, records, and stakeholder narratives.

## Cadence
Portfolio: quarterly. Product: monthly. Bet-level: weekly/biweekly.

## Decision Rights
Later→Next: product lead. Next→Now: product + engineering lead. Pause: bet owner recommends. Kill: product lead decides. Add to Later: any stakeholder proposes.

## Review Inputs
Updated roadmap, bet summaries, dependency health, capacity report, killed/paused log. Portfolio: add cross-product deps, capacity overview, scenario review.

## Outputs
Review record, updated roadmap, updated bet records, stakeholder communication (executive/team/external).

## Anti-Patterns
Review without decisions → end every item with decision or "no change." No decision log → record everything. One-size communication → tailor. Cadence drift → calendar-lock. No evidence → require updates. Silence after kill → communicate with context.
