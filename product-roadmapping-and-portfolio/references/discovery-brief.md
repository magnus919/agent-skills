## Non-goals

1. Now/Next/Later over timeline as default
2. Evidence/assumptions/commitments/options distinction in every artifact
3. Portfolio + individual product with same artifacts
4. Continue/pause/kill/revisit criteria mandatory
5. No framework duplication — routing only
