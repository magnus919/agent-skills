# Product Adoption

Diagnose and design product adoption — onboarding, activation, behavior change,
feature discovery, rollout segmentation, and sustained use — for consumer, B2B,
internal-tool, and public-service products.

## Why Install This Skill

Your agent can design an adoption plan anchored in evidence rather than marketing
assumptions. It distinguishes acquisition, activation, adoption, retention, and
expansion as separate stages with different owners and interventions. It knows
when to pause a rollout based on cohort activation evidence — and when to stop
an adoption effort entirely and feed that decision into lifecycle learning.

This skill works for products that are not SaaS subscriptions: internal tools,
public services, consumer apps, and non-subscription B2B products. It does not
assume NPS, conversion rates, or MRR benchmarks are universal.

## Quick Start

Load `SKILL.md` for the methodology overview and file map, then load specific
references and templates as the situation demands.

## Triggers

- Diagnosing low activation, slow time-to-value, or poor feature discovery
- Designing an adoption, onboarding, or rollout plan
- Segmenting users into rollout cohorts with evidence-based progression gates
- Reviewing sustained use after launch
- Designing in-product education, behavior-change interventions, or accessibility-aware flows
- Working with internal tools, public services, or non-SaaS products

## Requirements

No technical dependencies. Behavioral frameworks from Fogg Behavior Model,
COM-B, and evidence-based education design. Consumes adoption evidence from
product analytics but does not own instrumentation.
