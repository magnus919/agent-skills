# Discovery Brief — Product Adoption

Bounded discovery for issue #191. Maps existing skill landscape relevant to product adoption.

## Existing Material Survey

| Skill | Relationship |
|---|---|
| `go-to-market` | Upstream. Owns acquisition pipeline and PLG metrics. Product-adoption starts after user arrival. |
| `product-strategy` | Sibling. Sets direction; does not own adoption mechanics. |
| `product-methodology` | Sibling. Provides prioritization frameworks. |
| `product-discovery` | Sibling. Identifies adoption problems; does not design solutions. |
| `product-design-and-ux` | Sibling. Implements adoption flows; product-adoption defines strategy. |

## Gaps This Skill Fills

| Gap | Response |
|---|---|
| No skill distinguishes acquisition/activation/adoption/retention/expansion | `references/adoption-lifecycle-concepts.md` |
| No adoption for non-SaaS (internal tools, public services) | `references/non-saas-adoption-contexts.md` |
| No evidence-based adoption decision points | Decision table in SKILL.md; embedded in templates |
| No systematic behavior-change and education coverage | `references/behavior-change-and-education.md` |
| No cohort-based rollout segmentation | `templates/segmentation-and-rollout-record.md` |

## Ownership Boundaries

**OWNS:** Activation diagnostics, adoption strategy, rollout segmentation, behavior-change design, in-product education, accessibility-first adoption, evidence-based decision points, sustained-use review.

**Does NOT own:** Acquisition campaigns (go-to-market), analytics instrumentation (product-analytics-and-measurement), customer-success account management (conditional-customer-success), lifecycle decisions (product-lifecycle-learning), roadmap prioritization (product-roadmapping-and-portfolio).

## Non-SaaS Commitment

Explicitly supports internal tools, public services, consumer apps, and non-subscription B2B. Does not assume NPS, conversion, or MRM benchmarks are universal.
