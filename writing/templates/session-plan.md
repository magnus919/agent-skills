## The ritual
1. Recopy the last 20–30 words.
2. Write as fast as you can without editing (flow sprint; ~1,200+ words/hour is the low-water mark).
3. Do not re-read; do not correct; leave gaps marked.
4. Stop mid-scene or mid-sentence (never at a chapter boundary).
