# Kodak Charmera / Keychain Camera Plus

Accessed 2026-10-29. Primary source: [Kodak Charmera / Keychain Camera Plus project documentation](https://github.com/benbadio/keychain-camera-plus). Findings below are source-attributed; no hardware reproduction was performed in this investigation.

## Project claim

The toolkit identifies **Generalplus GPCV1235F** hardware and claims a microSD-installed UVC webcam mode while retaining battery-powered standalone capture. This is a specific Charmera firmware project, not evidence for Kodak-branded cameras in general.

## Limits

The developer reports hardware testing and a stock restoration procedure. This investigation did not establish a complete retail revision/firmware matrix, independent recovery results, UVC output quality or authenticity of redistributed stock images. No camera was flashed.

## Next evidence

Record board identity, existing firmware, exact input/output hashes and stock image provenance before evaluating a build. Determine whether retail collaborations or later revisions change the firmware layout. An update through the built-in loader remains a persistent firmware operation even when no soldering is needed.
