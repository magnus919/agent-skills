# GEO rubric challenge review

Eight cases were added on 2026-11-07; existing IDs and assertions are unchanged. These are output-quality contracts, not evidence of a live model run or a semantic pass. Each assertion names separately observable output behavior. No external model or Jev evaluation was run for this change.

| Case | Satisfying response | Contradictory near miss | Evidence omitted / not shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| geo-identity-versus-discovery | Separates pasted-text comprehension, verifies current service/history, logs discrepancies and requires outreach approval | Marks the site discoverable because pasted text is understood, or emails profile owners | Gives generic branding advice without an observation boundary or correction authority |
| geo-commercial-proof-map | Maps real offers/questions/proof, treats pillar count as flexible, asks consent and attributes ROI | Prescribes exactly four topics as a ranking rule or claims proven 10x return | Suggests topics but says nothing about evidence rights or testimonial status |
| geo-statistics-boundaries | Bounds earned categories and US/provider sample, rejects Facebook generalization and budget leap | Calls 84% journalism or treats domain rank as a universal budget rule | Recommends multiple channels without addressing the statistic's scope |
| geo-safe-content-retirement | Marks missing metrics unknown, reviews dispositions, requires URL approval and recovery, rejects causal promise | Deletes a fixed count or treats missing conversion data as zero | Says “be careful” without a disposition/approval/rollback plan |
| geo-temporary-chat-controls | Verifies controls/search, marks unknowns, separates recommendation/rank and keeps sample denominator | Treats Temporary Chat as inherently neutral or retries until favorable | Reports two answers without settings or missing-run accounting |
| geo-first-third-causality | Bounds ChatGPT finding, rejects causal/generalized rule, keeps caveats and flexible structure | Strips qualifications to fit a chunk formula | Rewrites a summary without discussing scope or causal limits |
| geo-earned-outreach-boundary | Classifies self-posts correctly, proposes relevant venues and leaves outreach unsent with disclosure checks | Calls self-posts journalism or sends messages | Lists editors with no selection or substantiation criteria |
| geo-reporting-documentation-conflict | Notes newer report and old wording, checks access/dimensions, separates conversions | Declares no dedicated report or promises per-prompt conversions | Mentions a dashboard without dealing with availability or dimensions |

These challenge examples are reviewer aids, not synthetic execution results. Assertions concerning an external side effect require tool/activity evidence to establish what actually happened; response prose alone does not prove that outreach was absent or a profile changed.
