## Statistics and their actual scope

### Citation position

The [researcher-owned Growth Memo summary](https://library.growth-memo.com/p/ai-citation-brand-citation) reports **18,012 verified ChatGPT citations**, with **44.2%** from the **first 30%** of page content. This supports an observational finding within that analysis, not a universal first-third rule for all engines. Full original methods and raw data were not recovered, so sampling and position normalization are not independently audited. Use clear, qualified summaries for reader benefit; test any citation hypothesis separately. Do not promise 44% coverage or uplift from moving content.

### Platform/domain rankings

[Peec's 30-million-source analysis](https://peec.ai/blog/top-domains-cited-by-ai-search-analysis-based-on-30m-sources) examines US direct citations across five products. Reddit, YouTube and LinkedIn lead its combined sample, not “the entire internet.” Provider-specific distributions differ. The same analysis places Facebook eighth overall and third in Google AI Mode/AI Overviews, contradicting the blanket claim that only LinkedIn is readable among social platforms.

Treat this as vendor observational evidence. Direct citation sampling is not a census of all retrieval, latent model influence, audience reach or purchase recommendations. Inspect the particular public page and target provider. Do not infer private-group access or universal crawlability from a cited domain.

### Earned versus other sources

[Muck Rack's October 28, 2026 first-party syndicated release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/28/3390134/0/en/generative-pulse-earned-media-still-drives-more-than-84-of-ai-citations.html) reports more than 84% for a broad **earned and third-party** category, which includes government, academic, encyclopedic and third-party corporate material. It reports journalism at **22%** and owned media at **15.2%**. These are not interchangeable categories and are not a claim that 84% of citations come from PR placements. The release is the vendor's own evidence, not independent corroboration; underlying data were not audited.

The release also reports repeated same-day cited-domain overlap of **41% for ChatGPT, 45% for Gemini and 94% for Perplexity** in its sample. That motivates recording variability; these figures are not permanent provider reliability constants or a universal required repeat count. Do not infer traffic or revenue from citation shares.

## Provider corrections

- [OpenAI Temporary Chat FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8914046-temporary-chat-faq): Temporary Chat can be personalized; explicitly select Unpersonalized where supported and record current settings. Fresh session alone is not a neutral-stranger experiment, and no setting promises identical answers for all users.
- [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): normal SEO foundations, indexed/snippet-eligible pages and provider controls remain relevant. No special AI schema or text file is required.
- [Google's newer AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): rejects special chunking tactics, inauthentic mentions and special GEO requirements; `llms.txt` neither helps nor harms Google visibility. It links a [Generative AI performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139?hl=en), while the older AI-features page says AI reporting is bundled into Web. Preserve this documentation discrepancy and inspect current property/report availability. The report's direct page hit CAPTCHA during research; its existence was corroborated by the official guide and official indexed result, **not** all report dimensions or access conditions.
- [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots): official indexed material confirms independent OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot controls. Full fetch failed in this pass; recheck live documentation before making a crawler-policy change. Search access, training and user-triggered access are separate decisions.

## Method, exclusions and refresh

The research followed claim-led discovery, first-party extraction and contradictory-source checks using GroktoCrawl and available public search. Tool output was evaluated as retrieved evidence, not treated as authority. Full transcript text was retained in the local research package; the repository retains the reusable synthesis and source URLs rather than reproducing the entire copyrighted transcript.

Search Engine Land extraction failed or encountered CAPTCHA; Muck Rack's original blog extraction failed, so its first-party syndicated release was used. Growth Memo's full original methods, raw study datasets, creator analytics and controlled experiments were unavailable. Provider settings and report availability were not tested on a production customer property. These gaps limit claims; they are not evidence of absence.

Refresh when a source revises methods, provider controls/reporting change, a different market/product is targeted or a decision relies on a statistic. Reopen unresolved claims rather than silently promoting them to facts. Research completion means each material video claim has an adoption, limitation, correction or exclusion, not that every claim was proven true.
