## What the examples support

The proposed commercial unit is a specific service in a geography. A service/city matrix creates candidate opportunities; a real provider monetizes leads through rent, fees, revenue share or delivery. The presenter recommends proving one before expanding. Static output, exact-match domains and independent tracking are choices to evaluate, not universal requirements.

The build separates planning/design references from coding, with an instruction bundle and site architecture, core/service/location/supporting pages and internal navigation. The reported result includes 46 pages, with four services and 26 locations, and a subsequent checking/revision pass. These are demonstration counts, not recommended quotas. It shows unwanted graphics, thin/mobile issues and fictional licensing, financing and testimonials that the presenter identifies for correction. Do not describe this as a completed production deployment. Calls/forms are discussed but end-to-end commissioning is not demonstrated. The report of 53 minutes of agent work does not prove a production-ready site in twelve minutes.

## What is still unverified

The “Microsite Blueprint for Claude Code” ZIP is described as previously downloaded from Academy / Microsite Empire; publicly described files include CLAUDE.md, index and architecture. A crawl of both descriptions, linked resources and build comments found no authorized public archive. Its full instructions, dependencies, license and current access conditions remain unverified. No trial or purchase was made. Membership marketing is not evidence that a trial immediately unlocks that archive.

No independently verified thousand-site profitable portfolio, representative conversion/revenue/cost distribution, sustained demo rankings or cohort failure rate was established. Generating a site, making it accurate, indexing, ranking, qualifying leads, collecting revenue and retaining profit are separate claims. Do not transfer evidence from one to the others.

## Reusable and rejected patterns

Keep structured briefs, a sourced business/claim register, deliberate architecture, design references, versioned build inputs, independent technical/visual review, tested lead routing and bounded economics-driven pilots. See `references/local-and-national-search.md`, `references/site-portfolio-strategy.md` and `references/scaled-content-quality.md`.

Do not standardize hidden ownership, cloned city funnels, fake testimonials, arbitrary page counts, public deployment without approval, agent permission bypass or “no links means no network to catch.” Google explicitly discusses multiple sites hiding scaled low-value content. Many useful independent sites are not inherently prohibited; legitimacy must be assessed from purpose, evidence and user experience, not a name or site count.
