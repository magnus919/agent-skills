# Thread Room resident card — draft 0.4

## English

### Saturday desk recording

Bring your item to Thread Room on Saturday, from 10 a.m. to 12 noon. Ask the
desk volunteer to record it.

This card confirms desk recording during that Saturday time window only. It does
not confirm collection or delivery outside that window.

**Access:** A ground-floor drop-off or a helper for people who cannot use the
stairs has not been confirmed.

**Friday:** Friday-evening collection has been proposed, but it is not
confirmed.

**Acceptance:** Recording an item at the desk is confirmed. This card does not
guarantee that an item is accepted before the resident leaves.

## Français

### Enregistrement au comptoir le samedi

Apportez votre objet à Thread Room le samedi, de 10 h à midi. Demandez à la
personne bénévole à l’accueil de l’enregistrer.

Cette carte confirme uniquement l’enregistrement au comptoir pendant cette plage
horaire du samedi. Elle ne confirme ni collecte ni livraison en dehors de cette
plage.

**Accès :** Un dépôt au rez-de-chaussée ou l’aide d’une personne pour celles et
ceux qui ne peuvent pas prendre les escaliers n’est pas confirmé.

**Vendredi :** Une collecte le vendredi soir a été proposée, mais elle n’est pas
confirmée.

**Acceptation :** L’enregistrement d’un objet à l’accueil est confirmé. Cette
carte ne garantit pas que l’objet sera accepté avant le départ de la personne
résidente.

## Checks before relying on the card

### Checkable in this draft

- English and French both state the Saturday 10 a.m.–12 noon window.
- Both versions distinguish desk recording from collection, delivery, and item
  acceptance.
- Neither version treats a ground-floor drop-off, helper, or Friday collection
  as available.
- The card stays within the supplied Thread Room commitments and does not name
  an unconfirmed service or contact route.

### Residents must test or confirm locally

- Whether the room and desk can actually be reached during the Saturday window,
  including the stairs/access barrier raised in the feedback.
- Whether a desk volunteer is present and can record the item as described.
- What confirmation, if any, the desk gives that the item was recorded, and
  whether the item is accepted before the resident leaves.
- Whether an accessible ground-floor option or a Friday collection is later
  confirmed. Neither should be relied on from this card alone.
