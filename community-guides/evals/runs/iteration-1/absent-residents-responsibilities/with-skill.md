# Oak Terrace responsibility map and coordination guide

_Fictional 18-household block. Draft for a resident-approved pilot._

## What this guide covers

Oak Terrace wants two small, shared capabilities:

1. keep a printed contact board current by replacing dated paper weekly; and
2. check privately whether residents received a seasonal guide and can request
   another copy or an accommodation.

This guide does not publish anyone's reason for being absent, require a
household to help, or treat attendance, silence, or a failed contact attempt as
consent. A role becomes operational only after the named person explicitly
accepts it for a stated period and agrees on a contact route.

The resident-facing board should show only the role, coverage status, next
review point, and a resident-approved way to volunteer or request help. It
should not show absence labels, reasons, diagnoses, household-level receipt
status, or private phone numbers without explicit opt-in.

## Status key

- **Confirmed:** the person accepted this specific role and its limited scope.
- **Proposed:** a person is being invited because the fixture suggests a
  possible fit; no duty exists yet.
- **Unfilled:** no person has accepted the role.
- **Declined:** the person explicitly said no. A nonresponse is not a decline.

The fixture confirms only that resident-a opted into board updates. Resident-d
is willing to review by text, but has not chosen a role. The other listed
residents are not assigned. These labels are coordinator notes, not material
for the public board.

## Responsibility map

| Role | Primary | Backup | Current status | Acceptance step and safe fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Board steward: replace dated paper weekly | Invite resident-a to choose this small task; weekday mornings appear compatible, but board-update opt-in is not role acceptance | Unfilled | Proposed primary; unfilled backup | Ask resident-a privately for a yes/no decision and a preferred week. If not accepted, leave the role unfilled and invite another opt-in resident; do not appoint an absent household. |
| Backup steward: cover a missed week | Unfilled | Unfilled | Unfilled | Seek a separate opt-in. The backup accepts only a missed-week cover, not an open-ended obligation. If no backup accepts, mark the board “update pending” rather than implying coverage. |
| Delivery checker: offer a private opt-in receipt check | Invite resident-d by text to choose this role or pass; text-only contact is an accommodation, not an assignment | Unfilled | Proposed invitation; unfilled backup | Ask whether resident-d wants this role, what reply window is workable, and whether another format is needed. No role is currently declined in the fixture. Do not convert silence, absence, or
an unreliable contact attempt into “declined.” Record “not reached” privately,
with no reason attached.

### Primary/backup rules

1. Ask for a specific role, small time window, contact method, and end or review
   point. A person may accept one role and decline another without explanation.
2. A primary and backup must be different willing households unless the group
   explicitly agrees that one person can safely cover both; no one is expected
   to be continuously available.
3. A backup is contacted only when the primary says they cannot cover or misses
   the agreed confirmation point. The backup does not become a permanent
   substitute without a new opt-in.
4. If either slot is empty, publish the gap as unfilled. The board must never
   imply that a role is covered because a name was suggested.
5. A resident may pass, ask for a different channel, or withdraw later. Remove
   their name from shared materials promptly and retain only the minimum
   aggregate pilot note needed to improve the process.

## Resident-facing coordination guide

### What residents can expect

- A dated paper copy is replaced weekly when a board steward has accepted that
  duty.
- If the steward cannot cover, a separately opted-in backup may cover one
  missed week.
- A delivery checker may ask privately whether you received the seasonal guide.
  You can answer “received,” “please provide another copy,” “I need a different
  format or route,” or “I do not want to answer.” No answer is required.
- Requests are handled without asking for a diagnosis or the reason someone is
  absent. Choosing not to share does not affect access to the guide.
- A resident can volunteer, decline, request an accommodation, or withdraw
  later without penalty.

### How to volunteer or request help

Use the resident-approved route shown on the board, or ask for a private
conversation. State only what is needed:

> “I can be primary for [role] during [time period]. My preferred contact route
> is [route]. Please confirm the review date.”

or:

> “I can be backup for one missed week, but not an ongoing role.”

or:

> “I need the guide through [format or route]. I do not want to discuss why.”

It is acceptable to say “not available” without giving a reason. If contact is
unreliable, the record should say only “not reached” or “awaiting reply.” Do not
leave repeated messages that pressure someone to respond.

### Private receipt-check procedure

The delivery checker, if accepted, sends one concise invitation through the
resident's agreed route and one optional reminder. The checker records only an
aggregate result for the pilot:

| Result | Shared aggregate note |
|---|---|
| Received | Count only, if useful |
| Needs another copy or format | Count only; route the request privately |
| Declined to answer | Count only, if useful |
| Not reached | Count only; do not infer a reason |

Names, household numbers, reasons for absence, diagnoses, and message contents
stay out of the shared board and group notes.

## One-cycle community pilot

This is one bounded pilot, not a seven-session program. It tests reachability and
willingness before any role is treated as operational.

### Prepare

1. Make a plain, dated sample seasonal guide and a draft board notice. Include
   the role statuses “proposed” and “unfilled”; do not print resident reasons.
2. Privately invite resident-a to accept or decline the board-steward role for
   one defined replacement cycle. Do not treat the existing board-update opt-in
   as acceptance.
3. Privately invite resident-d by text to choose either delivery checker or
   accessibility liaison, or to pass. Offer a reply format that works with
   text-only contact.
4. Invite a separate backup steward and an escalation owner. The invitation
   must describe the small scope and review point. Do not fill either role by
   assumption.
5. Choose a private, resident-approved place for any temporary aggregate notes.
   Obtain permission before recording individual feedback.

### Run the pilot

- The accepted primary, if any, replaces the dated paper once. If no primary
  accepts, stop short of claiming board coverage and test only the invitation
  and reachability steps.
- Ask the accepted backup, if any, to confirm whether they can cover one named
  missed-week scenario. This is a confirmation test, not a demand that they
  perform extra work.
- The accepted delivery checker, if any, offers the private receipt check. The
  accepted accessibility liaison, if any, collects format or channel requests
  without diagnoses. One person may choose only one of these roles unless they
  explicitly accept both scopes.
- Keep the contact board public-facing and minimal. Put private responses in no
  public list.

### Observe and decide

The pilot host records only whether each step was possible:

| Check | Evidence to retain | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Reachability | Reply, no reply, or requested accommodation; no reason | Keep, change route, or mark not reached |
| Willingness | Explicit accept, explicit decline, or no decision | Confirm, record declined, or keep proposed/unfilled |
| Primary task | Dated paper replaced or task could not be attempted | Operational for the agreed window, or leave unfilled |
| Backup handoff | Backup confirmed, declined, or not reached | Keep backup, seek another, or publish gap |
| Private receipt check | Aggregate counts and barriers only | Revise delivery route or keep current route |

After the pilot, update the private map and publish only confirmed roles,
unfilled roles, the next review point, and the approved way to request help.
If there is no accepted primary, backup, private receipt-check route, or safe
escalation path, the corresponding function remains unfilled. That is a valid
pilot result.

### Pilot invitation template — prepare, do not send without authorization

> Oak Terrace is testing one small coordination cycle for the printed board and
> seasonal guide. Would you like to (a) accept [specific role] for [defined
> period], (b) review the task and decide later, or (c) pass? You do not need to
> explain. Your preferred reply route is [route]. We will not publish your
> absence status, reason, or private response.

## Agent checks and evidence limits

The draft was checked against the supplied fictional fixture and the community-
guide method:

- both requested capabilities have a visible activity, output, next action, and
  check;
- proposed, confirmed, unfilled, and declined states are separated;
- resident-a's board-update opt-in is not inflated into role acceptance;
- resident-d's text-only accommodation is respected without assigning a role;
- absent or unknown residents are not appointed and absence reasons are not
  exposed;
- primary, backup, receipt-check, accessibility, and escalation fallbacks are
  explicit; and
- the pilot tests reachability and willingness before operational reliance.

These are agent checks only. No resident pilot, consent collection, council
contact, translation review, or external publication has occurred. Do not claim
participant-tested usability until the one-cycle pilot produces evidence.

## Maintenance

- **Edition:** 0.1 pilot draft
- **Maintainer:** unconfirmed; an owner must opt in before the guide is posted
- **Review trigger:** after the pilot, when a role changes, or when the seasonal
  guide or contact route changes
- **Record rule:** keep only the minimum private acceptance and aggregate pilot
  notes; residents may correct or withdraw shared information
- **Revision route:** the accepted maintainer updates the private map, marks the
  revision date, and gives residents the revised paper copy or approved format

The next revision should report which roles were accepted, which remained
unfilled, which contact or access barriers appeared, and what specific change
will be checked in the next cycle. It should not add explanations for why any
resident was absent.
