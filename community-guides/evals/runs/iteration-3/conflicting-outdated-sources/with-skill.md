## Resident-facing draft

### Current status

The older printed notice dated **March 10, 2023** names the **basement laundry room** as the assembly point. A newer building update dated **February 14, 2026** reports that construction closed the basement and names the **north parking-lot gate** instead.

The north-gate route is **not yet confirmed for current use**. The supplied information does not establish that the route is open and safe at all times.

**This route-specific guide is therefore not published yet.** The 2023 basement instruction should not be republished as the current assembly point, and this draft does not invent an interim route. A replacement notice will be issued only after the route, sign, and an accessible alternative have been verified with the building’s responsible person.

## Decision

**Pause publication now.** The evidence reconciles the historical change in plan, but not the current implementation:

- The 2026 management update is newer and reports that the basement location is closed.
- The same update proposes the north parking-lot gate as the replacement location.
- The March 2026 resident observations expose a gap between that proposed plan and conditions residents may encounter.
- No supplied source confirms that the north-gate route is currently open and safe at all times.

This is a publication hold, not a finding that the north gate is always blocked or unsafe. The observations establish a current concern, not an all-times condition.

### What is and is not reconciled

The older and newer notices can be reconciled as a dated change: the basement instruction is historical, and the 2026 update reports a newer intended location. They cannot be silently blended into a single confirmed instruction. The resident observations do not overturn the management update, but they make its implementation unresolved. The active question is whether the named north-gate route is currently open, signed, and usable, including for residents with mobility needs.

## Verification gate before publication

Publication may proceed only after these checks produce a usable, dated record:

1. **Inspect the route and sign.** Check the north parking-lot route and whether the sign is present and usable.
2. **Confirm with the building’s responsible person.** Confirm the current intended assembly point and the condition represented by the resident notice.
3. **Check accessibility.** Ask residents with mobility needs to test the route or describe an accessible alternative.

If these checks do not establish a route that can be described accurately, keep publication paused. Do not turn an unverified proposal into a resident instruction. The responsible publication owner is not identified in the packet and remains unconfirmed.
