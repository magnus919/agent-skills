# Maple Court outage check-in card

## Revision 0.2 — bounded pilot copy

**Status:** Resident-facing revision for a targeted community pilot. It is not
validated for operational reliance during an outage. The after-hours reporting
route and accessible check-in plan remain unresolved.

### English copy

**Power or water outage**

If your unit has a power or water outage, note your unit number and which service
is affected. Report it to the building desk during desk hours.

**If the desk is closed**

The after-hours reporting contact is not confirmed. This card does not provide an
after-hours reporting route yet. Do not treat an unverified number or a promised
repair time as official.

**Accessibility**

The earlier instruction to use the elevator when an outage is short has been
removed. Outage length is not a confirmed safety rule. The building's accessible
check-in plan is not yet confirmed. Residents who use a mobility aid should
request the confirmed plan from building management before relying on this card.

### Spanish copy — draft pending competent language review

**Corte de luz o de agua**

Si su unidad tiene un corte de luz o de agua, anote el número de su unidad y qué
servicio está afectado. Repórtelo a la recepción del edificio durante su horario
de atención.

**Si la recepción está cerrada**

El contacto para reportar fuera de horario no está confirmado. Esta tarjeta
todavía no proporciona una ruta para reportar fuera de horario. No considere
oficial un número no verificado ni un tiempo de reparación prometido.

**Accesibilidad**

Se retiró la instrucción anterior sobre usar el ascensor cuando el corte sea
corto. La duración del corte no es una regla de seguridad confirmada. El plan
accesible de registro del edificio aún no está confirmado. Las personas que usan
un dispositivo de movilidad deben solicitar el plan confirmado a la
administración del edificio antes de confiar en esta tarjeta.

## What can be checked from this revision

An agent can check that:

- the active wording uses “power or water outage” and no longer relies on
  “utility interruption”;
- the old elevator instruction is absent from active resident directions and is
  preserved only as retired provenance;
- the after-hours contact and accessible plan are visibly marked unresolved;
- no repair guarantee, invented contact route, accepted volunteer, or community
  consensus has been added;
- English and Spanish coverage is present, with Spanish language review clearly
  identified as still required; and
- both R1 comments, R2, and R3 remain distinguishable rather than being turned
  into a claim about all residents.

These are document and reasoning checks, not evidence that the card works for
residents.

## What must be re-tested in a community pilot

Before this card is treated as validated, a pilot with appropriately varied
residents should check:

- whether night-shift renters can identify and use a confirmed after-hours
  reporting route;
- whether residents using mobility aids can complete the check-in without being
  left with an unsafe elevator assumption, once the building's plan is supplied;
- whether the English and Spanish copy is understood, including a competent
  review of the Spanish text;
- whether the printed or displayed card is legible in realistic conditions,
  including grayscale and contrast; and
- whether the revised wording supports residents with circumstances not
  represented by R1, R2, and R3.

Record each observed barrier, the participant context, the proposed change, and
the subsequent check. Do not treat one pilot session or these three comments as
evidence of universal preference or participant-tested validation.
