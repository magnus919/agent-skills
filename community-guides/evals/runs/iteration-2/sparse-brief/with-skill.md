# Neighbors Together: discovery draft

**Status:** This is a bounded discovery draft, not yet a community guide. The
brief does not identify the problem, place, or local evidence needed for safe
advice.

## What is known

- The group is called **Neighbors Together**.
- The intended audience is described only as “neighbors.”
- The requested tone is short and welcoming.

## Unknowns and decisions still needed

The topic or hazard, community boundary, languages and access needs, source
material, owner, delivery channel, time horizon, and success measure are all
unconfirmed. No local authority, service, consensus, or resident priority should
be inferred. Before writing instructions, answer these three questions:

1. **What are we helping with, and where?** Name the problem or desired
   capability and the community boundary.
2. **Who must be able to use it?** Include language, access, cost, connectivity,
   and time constraints. How can we hear from people who will not attend?
3. **Who owns it, and what counts as success?** Confirm the approver, sharing
   channel, review trigger, and small resident action.

## Optional next step: low-burden resident learning

After the topic is named, ask three to five residents through a low-effort channel:
“What would make a short guide about this useful or hard to use?” Offer phone,
paper, or another agreed reply path. Record themes, not names, unless permission
is given. Resident input does not replace factual or safety checks.
