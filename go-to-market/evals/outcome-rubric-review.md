# Outcome-partnership rubric challenge examples

Added 2026-11-06. Existing case IDs and assertions are unchanged. These are authored
rubric checks, not recorded model outputs, independent ground truth or a semantic
release pass. Each row tests whether an assertion distinguishes met, not met and
not shown. Omission snippets are deliberately incomplete, not incorrect answers.

## outcome-partnership-uncontrolled-sale

| Assertion | Satisfying example | Contradiction | Omission example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Separates a lead or appointment deliverable from a managed operating outcome. | An attended appointment is a narrower deliverable than delivered and paid work. | An inquiry and a paid job are interchangeable outcomes. | The response discusses the service offer without addressing this requirement. |
| Identifies the missing outcome visibility as a reason to hold or narrow the proposed pricing basis. | Without auditable sales/payment evidence, hold the outcome-linked offer or price a verifiable narrower service. | Charge completed-sale commission even with no access to sale evidence. | The response discusses the service offer without addressing this requirement. |
| Assigns accountable parties and operator authority separately for response, quoting, delivery and collection. | The operator owns response and may qualify/schedule within agreed capacity; the client owns quoting and approves prices while the operator drafts estimates only. The client owns delivery with no operator authority over licensed work. The client owns collection; the operator may reconcile evidence, not debit customers. | The operator is accountable for all outcomes even though the client retains all authority. | The response discusses the service offer without addressing this requirement. |
| Treats missing response commitments as an unresolved operating dependency. | Require a response owner, measurable service level and remedy before promising sales. | No response commitment is needed because payment is performance based. | The response discusses the service offer without addressing this requirement. |

## Trigger probes (manual routing checks, not output-quality evals)

Should trigger: “Compare lead sales with managed outcome-linked partnerships”; “Design a bounded revenue-share acquisition pilot”; “Decide who controls quoting and collections before choosing our acquisition offer.”

Should not trigger as primary: “Reverse a Stripe transfer” (stripe operations); “Interpret the legality of this healthcare referral contract” (legal-strategy/qualified counsel).
