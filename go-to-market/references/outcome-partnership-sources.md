# Outcome-partnership source and claim ledger

Reviewed 2026-11-06. These public sources support bounded claims. Product docs
verify their own mechanism, not independent business performance. Recheck current
rules, jurisdiction and facts before any operational decision. No private
interviews or customer data underpin this reference.

The model ladder, role matrix, reconciliation controls and pilot gates are an
analytical synthesis, not quotations or a validated contract. The review found
no direct comparable cohort proving revenue-share microsite partnerships beat
lead sales. Demand, partner acquisition cost, sustainable pricing, causal lift,
retention and repeatability remain open empirical questions. Vendor blogs and
search-only snippets were not used to establish typical rates, market size,
legal permission or a proven competitive advantage.
