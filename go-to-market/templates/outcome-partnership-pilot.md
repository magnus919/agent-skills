# Outcome-partnership pilot decision record

A planning record, not a legal agreement or authorization to contact customers.
Fill unknowns explicitly; unresolved legal/control gates keep the work at research
or draft status.

## Decision and evidence

- Date, decision owner, evidence reviewed, next review:
- Service, buyer, end customer, geography/jurisdiction, exclusions:
- Actual buyer problem/current alternative and observed evidence:
- Selected model and why; narrower model if gates fail:
- Compared offers on like scope (fixed / hybrid / outcome-linked):
- Paid commitment/data cooperation evidence; disconfirming findings:
- Source claims and limits; assumptions still untested:
