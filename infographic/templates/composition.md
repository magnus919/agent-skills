# Composition specification

## Communication
Audience, task, takeaway, actual viewing context and dimensions.

## Content layers
Exact headline and qualifier; ordered supporting claims; annotations; sources.
Associate each factual claim with its source and any transformation. Mark all
illustrative material explicitly.

## Structure
Dominant semantic layout; entry point; reading order; groups; focal point;
meaning of connectors, containment, numbering, scales and color.

## Visual language
Selected recipe; type roles; palette and redundant cues; spacing rhythm;
illustration treatment; quiet zones for exact text; prohibited distortions.

## Production
Available renderer and inspection route; deterministic chart/text layer;
illustration layer; export format; editable source and linked assets.

## Acceptance
Correct calculations/geometry; verified labels; readable final-size export;
no overlap/crop; accessible equivalent; traceable sources; honest review note.
