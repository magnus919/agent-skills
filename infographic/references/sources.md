# Foundations and reuse

Research consulted 2026-10-16. The workflow, layout decision tables, fourteen-style
taxonomy, prompt fragments and templates are original synthesis. They are not a
validated universal recipe or an endorsement by the organizations below. Sources
support specific principles and examples; they do not license copying their artwork,
branding or prose. No third-party artwork is bundled.

## Research foundations

- [Munzner, A Nested Model for Visualization Design and Validation (2009)](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/labs/imager/tr/2009/NestedModel/):
  separate domain/task, abstraction, encoding and implementation decisions and their
  validation. Adapted here to avoid polishing the wrong communication problem.
- [Heer and Bostock, Crowdsourcing Graphical Perception (2010)](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/2010/crowd-perception/heer-chi2010.pdf):
  empirical graphical-perception tasks inform encoding choices. This does not imply
  a universal ranking independent of audience, task and context, or a ban on pie charts.
- [Borkin et al., Beyond Memorability: Visualization Recognition and Recall (2015)](https://vcg.seas.harvard.edu/publications/20150101-beyond-memorability-visualization-recognition-and-recall):
  recognition/recall are distinct concerns. Illustration is not automatically harmful
  or beneficial; assess its contribution to this explanation rather than maximizing decoration.

## Practical communication and chart guidance

- [Datawrapper chart guide](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/chart-types-guide): task-sensitive chart selection.
- [Datawrapper bar/column baselines](https://www.datawrapper.de/academy/why-our-column-and-bar-charts-start-at-zero): magnitude encoded by length.
- [Datawrapper text in visualization](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/text-in-data-visualizations): explanatory copy and annotation.
- [Nielsen Norman Group visual hierarchy](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visual-hierarchy-ux-definition/) and
  [infographic design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/designing-effective-infographics/): reader attention and organization; practitioner guidance, not universal font/count rules.
- [ONS uncertainty guidance](https://service-manual.ons.gov.uk/data-visualisation/guidance/showing-uncertainty-in-charts): meaningful uncertainty presentation.
  Institutional preferences are conventions, not universal prohibitions. Interval overlap
  alone is not a definitive significance test.

## Visual vocabulary and public interpretation

These are primary design guides or institutional collections that inform the
range of treatments, not sources to imitate verbatim.

- [IBM illustration overview](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/illustration/overview/) and
  [people](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/illustration/people/): coherent illustration systems and representation.
- [NPS pictographic symbols](https://www.nps.gov/maps/web/tools/park-tiles/pictographic-symbols/index.html): purposeful symbol systems.
- [University of Reading typography collections](https://www.reading.ac.uk/typography/collections-and-archives): historical information-design and typographic practice.
- [V&A paper-art discussion](https://www.vam.ac.uk/blog/caring-for-our-collections/neither-printed-painted-nor-drawn): material visual vocabulary.
- [NPS wayside exhibit design](https://home.nps.gov/subjects/hfc/wayside-exhibit-design.htm) and
  [planning](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/hfc/wayside-exhibit-planning.htm): interpretation in a real viewing environment.
- [Library of Congress WPA poster collection](https://www.loc.gov/collections/works-progress-administration-posters/about-this-collection/): historical poster composition examples.
- [NASA universe graphics](https://science.nasa.gov/universe/graphics/): conceptual scientific illustration examples.
- [NPS map symbols and patterns](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/gisandmapping/map-symbols-patterns-for-nps-maps.htm): geographic symbol vocabulary.

Notebook, comic and sculpted recipes are generic original design synthesis, not
attributed research findings. No recipe implies an exclusive association with a
profession, age, culture or subject.

## Accessibility standards and guidance

- [WCAG 2.2 text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)
- [WCAG 2.2 non-text contrast](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)
- [WCAG 2.2 use of color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)
- [WAI complex images tutorial](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/complex/)

Apply the appropriate criterion and exceptions to the actual delivery medium.
Contrast ratios alone do not establish overall accessibility.

## Rights and provenance

The skill's original text is MIT licensed under the repository license. For any
external asset, separately record creator, source URL, license/permission, required
credit and modifications. Check the specific asset; public availability and a
public institution's website are not blanket reuse permissions. Do not copy a
commercial illustrator's work, branded identity, proprietary template, or private
