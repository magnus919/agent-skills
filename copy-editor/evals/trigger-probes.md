# Trigger probes (manual design review)

Should load: “Copyedit this chapter and keep my voice”; “Make a style sheet and author queries”; “Check grammar and terminology consistency in our report.”

Should route elsewhere: “Plan next quarter's editorial calendar” (managing-editor); “Rewrite my novel's plot” (writers-helper); “Certify this website's accessibility” (web-accessibility).

These are boundary probes, not evidence of measured activation performance.
