# Research record and source-linked findings

Access date: 2026-11-15. Research question: what responsibilities, methods, judgment, communication and reusable artifacts enable professional copy editing across contexts, including bounded agent assistance? Destination: this tracked record, supporting references, templates and eval cases. Research uses English-language public professional standards and practitioner guidance; it is not an exhaustive survey of national practices or evidence of agent effectiveness.

## Method and inclusion

Used GroktoCrawl for a broad deep-agent investigation, targeted discovery of Editors Canada, CIEP and EFA guidance, source extraction, and follow-up on fiction and accessibility. Retained professional association standards as normative within their stated scope; practitioner guidance supplies examples rather than universal rules. Search snippets were discovery aids; extracted source content informed the synthesis. Source-level findings below preserve reusable claims without reproducing copyrighted articles. Templates and agent workflow are original synthesis.

## Decisions, exclusions and uncertainties

- The old CIEP fiction blog URL extracted a homepage; that content was excluded. A focused follow-up located the current resource URL.
- EFA and Editors Canada use related but nonidentical service boundaries. The skill resolves this by defining the actual commissioned tasks rather than assigning authority from a title.
- Professional standards cover human editorial work. Parallel agent passes, frozen inputs and lead integration are conservative workflow adaptations, not experimentally validated benefits.
- No source establishes that copyediting alone verifies scientific validity, legal clearance, permissions or complete accessibility. Those remain scoped specialist tasks.
- Copyrighted style manuals are not bundled. Users must provide or authorize access to applicable current rules; the skill does not claim subscription access.
- Multilingual and jurisdiction-specific work requires relevant language/domain expertise. The role map is an adaptable guide, not certification of competence in every listed context.

## Gap audit and improvements

Compared the draft against source responsibilities: correctness, consistency, accuracy, completeness, scope, communication, versions, audience, accessibility and non-introduction of errors are all routed to concrete workflow steps and templates. Follow-up addressed fiction continuity and accessibility. Added protected technical strings, uncertainty-preserving scientific edits, unresolved-query handoff and incomplete-component coverage to catch foreseeable agent failures. No automation script is included: all consequential edits need context and meaning review.
