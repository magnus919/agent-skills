# Copy Editor

Make writing correct, consistent and clear while preserving its meaning and voice.

## Why Install This Skill

A fluent rewrite can change a fact, flatten a character's voice, or hide an unresolved ambiguity. This skill helps your agent decide what to correct, what to ask the author, and what to leave alone. It supports books, fiction, news, scholarly and technical material, organizational writing, and digital content.

You get a repeatable editing process and usable records: a brief, style sheet, query log, and handoff checklist. When several contributors or agents help, the workflow keeps their proposals tied to one source version and gives a lead editor responsibility for integration.

## Quick Start

Ask: “Copyedit this article for UK English using our attached house style. Preserve voice and scientific uncertainty. Return visible edits, a style sheet and unresolved queries.” Supply the source version and specify whether fact verification and references are included.

## Triggers

- Copyedit a manuscript, report, article or digital text.
- Resolve inconsistent usage, citations, numbers or terminology.
- Prepare a style sheet, author queries or an editorial handoff.
- Coordinate bounded copyediting passes by contributors or agents.

## Requirements

No runtime dependency. Native tracked edits require a suitable document tool. Supply or obtain authorized access to the applicable house style and sources; specialist checks require appropriate expertise.
