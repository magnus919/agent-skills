# Incubation Log

A capture journal for deliberate breaks. Fragments that surface during a break
are raw material: write them down as they arrive, without judging or editing
them. Capture first; decide later.

## Break entry

Start a new entry for each deliberate break.

- Date: ____________
- Problem or question on my mind: ____________
- Focused effort before the break (what, how long): ____________
- Break activity: ____________
