# Conditions Audit

Reverse-engineer the conditions around your best work so you can reproduce
them. A good moment is probabilistic: rebuild the conditions that surrounded
it and you raise the odds of its return. Conditions are idiosyncratic —
discover yours by trial, not by imitation.
