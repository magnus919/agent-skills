## Findings

1. **Factory is the production system.** Define recurring demand, inputs, artifact flow, acceptance and delivery before selecting an agent topology. High confidence as a design distinction; general productivity benefit unproven. S1, S2, S3, S9.
2. **Coherent responsibility boundaries matter more than job titles.** Keep coupled decisions together; isolate work only when context, authority or verification benefits outweigh coordination. Medium confidence for transfer to arbitrary coding work. S1, S4, S5.
3. **Steering is a state transition.** Message delivery, acknowledgement, adoption and acceptance are different observations. Version contracts, invalidate affected downstream work and fence obsolete results. High confidence in the distinction; the proposed portable protocol is synthesis, not a reproduced runtime. S1, S6, S7.
4. **Quality and capacity are factory concerns.** Protected user scenarios and integrated checks precede delivery; throughput includes queueing, review, rework and failures. Model routing needs local evidence. High confidence in measurement boundaries, unknown local benefit. S2, S3, S4, S5, S8, S9.
5. **Evolution needs controlled promotion.** Propose role changes from recurring evidence, compare revisions and retain rollback. Do not equate self-authored rules or model judging with validated improvement. Medium confidence; this package makes no field-efficacy claim. S1, S2, S5.

## Evidence and contradictions

The video favors conversational, evolving coordination; Anthropic distinguishes adaptive orchestration from predictable workflows and warns about cost. StrongDM advocates non-interactive development without human code review, whereas Maher's demonstration retains conversational steering. These are different factory variants; neither is a universal autonomy requirement. MAST documents design, alignment and verification failures, challenging the idea that adding roles alone solves reliability. DORA's organizational findings caution against treating faster generation as delivered value.
