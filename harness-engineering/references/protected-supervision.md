# Protected supervision in harnesses

Use when designing consequential action or worker-launch boundaries. Consume the
ai-governance protected-control registry and the agent-production-operations
pre-execution control-integrity receipt. Security owns identities and enforcement;
evals owns monitor/evidence challenges. Do not duplicate those contracts.

Keep monitor configuration, alert routes, audit sinks, safety cases, ceilings,
kill switches, evaluator oracles, and gates outside the controlled agent's writable
authority. Include tool APIs, repository permissions, credentials, CI jobs, indirect
service identities, generated configuration, and child processes in reachability
analysis. A protected filename alone does not protect an API or deployment path.

Before launching each child, resolve its actual identity, environment, tool scope,
parent grant, and mandatory coverage. Inherit ceilings and evidence references;
never widen authority through delegation. Require a fresh independently verified
receipt bound to the child's identity and work scope. The runtime launcher, outside
the controlled agent's authority, rejects missing, expired, mismatched or revoked
receipts. Correlate parent/child events and detect orphaned or uncovered workers.
A parent heartbeat or successful task is not proof of child supervision.

Challenge an uncovered child, changed sink, stale receipt, deleted monitor, edited
oracle, and recovery that disables revocation in safe fixtures. Verify refusal at
the launch/action boundary and independent state effects; a worker's declaration
that it is monitored is insufficient. Missing, degraded, redirected, or suppressed
mandatory controls are control-integrity incidents: hold, contain or terminate
through operations, using a protected escalation route. Do not bypass the launcher
or reinterpret the control to finish the task.

Recovery must retain revocation and monitoring, invalidate stale grants and
receipts, reconcile partial effects, and obtain a fresh receipt before restart.
Complete only with boundary-level evidence or an explicit unverified gap; offline
fixtures and instructions alone do not prove live enforcement.
