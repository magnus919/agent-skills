# Capability admission record

Proposal until the named human approves it; admission alone grants no agent authority.
Use one record per effective component configuration, linking existing evidence rather than copying it.

- Record ID/revision/date; type (Skill / MCP / A2A / library / other):
- Purpose and consuming agents/environments; accountable internal owner; independent human approver:
- Source/maintainer; inspected source revision or digest; effective configuration snapshot/reference:
- Requested operations, identities/credential references, data classes, destinations and delegation:
- Existing approved alternatives, trigger/functional overlap, conflict resolution and reason to add:
- Evidence links, scope, collection date, reviewer, freshness/sufficiency criteria and expiry:
- Counterevidence, unknown provenance, missing/stale evidence and residual risks:
- Disposition: admit / admit with restrictions / hold / reject / update / disable / retire:
- Rationale, allowed scope, prohibited uses, conditions, accountable approval reference:
- Effective time, expiry/review date and event-triggered revisit conditions:
- Linked authority decision IDs and consuming grants; external enforcement owner and activation receipt:
- Update delta (old/new revision AND configuration), affected consumers/grants/evidence and re-review owner:
- Emergency disable/revocation path and owner; in-flight cancellation/reconciliation; credential response:
- Known-good rollback artifact/configuration; restoration decision and required verification:
- Replacement/retirement consumers, migration/fallback, retained records/data and revocation receipts:
- Missing evidence owner, next action/date, and operational handoff/completion evidence:

Attach deeper third-party diligence or agentic governance review only when relevant. Do not record
secret values. Mark approval, enforcement and exit receipts as pending when not observed.
