## Discovery, comparison, and exclusions

- Searched by video ID and title, then by the episode's specific claims. Used the publisher's indexed transcript and mediated captions because the shortlink fetch missed and the direct YouTube page did not expose a readable timed transcript. Third-party summaries oriented discovery and were excluded as corroboration.
- Followed pstack to Cursor's public repository and read exact files from the pinned revision. Traced Grok Bot identity through product, connector, and collaboration documentation. Searched NIST, the IETF Datatracker, and current vendor documentation for separate workload principals, delegated authorization, and succession. The artifact preserves dated sources and open questions rather than a copy of the episode.
- Used the pinned [research baseline](https://github.com/magnus919/agent-skills/commit/778a3a1f2a21fdd4a5fe108abd29047d82971143) to identify existing coverage. The [merged catalog PR](https://github.com/magnus919/agent-skills/pull/876) records the three applied changes. This package keeps the underlying research and proposed qualification cases with that provenance.
- Rechecked Team Bots connector selection and handoff behavior, Entra application and delegated tokens and sponsorship tasks, AWS token exchange, NCCoE's consultation and first proposed implementation, and the pinned OAuth draft. The recheck used public documentation. It did not replay the original transcript extraction or exercise a tenant.
- Excluded unverified claims about market adoption, subjective experience, the effectiveness of wider agent swarms, and universal savings from reducing review. Account-level tests, connector migration, PR-volume validation, feedback experiments, and a live pstack run were outside this research pass. The offboarding table is a proposed fixture for future validation.

The durable artifacts are the linked brief and this evidence ledger. They preserve the relevant attributed claims, primary-source comparisons, proposed qualification cases, and gaps.
