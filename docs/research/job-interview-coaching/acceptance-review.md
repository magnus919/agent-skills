## Remaining evidence boundaries

No human coach or domain expert adjudication, no real candidate outcome study, no measured causal effect on readiness or offers, and no exhaustive coverage of every specialty or jurisdiction. These are limits on the evidence, not missing basic candidate/interviewer functionality. The skill explicitly routes specialist correctness to current sources and appropriate review.
