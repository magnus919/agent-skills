## Design

One methodology skill selects candidate, interviewer, or both, then loads relevant format, profession, and responsibility-level references. Shared standards preserve truthful experience, exact transcript evidence, neutral questioning, and distinctions between missing evidence and demonstrated weakness.

The sixteen focused profession playbooks each include task selection, evidence, an original response contrast, neutral probes, a drill, a transfer question, level adaptations, and interviewer considerations. They support almost-any-profession adaptation by task and responsibility; they do not claim exhaustive specialty standards.

The former workflow-architect interviewer child remains consolidated into the parent's active-discovery reference. It is unrelated to employment interviewing and is no longer separately discoverable in the repository.

## Reading the validation

Twenty-six distinct scenario responses and three multi-turn sessions were actually executed, with synthetic inputs and saved outputs. A separate model-teacher reviewed all 88 distinct case assertions across an initial and delta review. This is advisory model evidence, not human adjudication, calibrated accuracy, or proof of user learning. Read the execution report before making any quality or outcome claim.
