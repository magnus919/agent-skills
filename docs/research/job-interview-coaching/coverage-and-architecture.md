# Coverage and architecture

The prototype is implemented in [job-interview-coaching](../../../job-interview-coaching/SKILL.md).

## Routing

Select candidate/interviewer/both, then the mode reference. Load additional format, responsibility, profession, and inclusion references only as needed. Shared evidence boundaries live in the entry point. No named service or private career skill is required.

## Coverage

| Family | Initial coverage | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Technical and creative | Software, data, engineering, product, design, academic research | Specialist technical validation still needs appropriate artifacts and experts |
| Operational | Trades, maintenance, manufacturing, logistics, service, administration | Conversation does not prove practical skill or safe equipment use |
| Care and regulated | Healthcare, care, education, legal practice | No clinical, safeguarding, licensing, or legal answer key |
| Business and public service | Consulting, sales, finance, management, public service | Employer and jurisdiction overlays must be checked |
| Responsibility | First job, apprentice, independent practitioner, senior IC, manager, director, executive | Actual accountability takes precedence over title |

## Artifacts

The skill includes session-brief, transcript-review, interview-plan, and practice-log templates. The eval manifest contains candidate and interviewer cases, profession differences, missing evidence, transcript contamination, inclusive practice, and multi-turn interaction requirements. Trigger probes are kept outside that manifest.

## Related cleanup

The parent retains active discovery, the observer and builder remain sub-skills, and the manifest points active discovery to the parent entry point. ## Acceptance boundaries

Structural and schema checks do not validate behavior. Representative task execution and domain review should challenge transcript grounding, truthful rewrites, neutral probes, missing-evidence handling, turn-taking, assistance tracking, and specialist limits. Record failures and untested areas rather than inventing review evidence.
