# Sources and research limits

The maintained annotated ledger is in [the skill research reference](../../../job-interview-coaching/references/research-and-sources.md). It records source contribution, access level, applicability, and remaining research needs. Public pages, abstracts, and discovery-only leads are distinguished.

This initial research informed a prototype; it does not establish coaching efficacy or validated occupational scoring. No private interview transcripts or personal career history were used.
