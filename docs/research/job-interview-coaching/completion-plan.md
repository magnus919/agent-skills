## Objective

Deliver a broadly useful, research-grounded skill for candidate preparation, expert transcript critique, interactive practice, and interviewer design/assessment. Preserve the completed consolidation of workflow-architect's old interviewer child. Do not use the owner's personal career history as the skill's basis.

## Work and acceptance criteria

1. **Practitioner and evidence research.** Examine public descriptions from at least four named coaching approaches, primary research beyond abstract-only discovery where accessible, and author/publisher reference materials. Record what was actually read, methodological limits, and concrete implementation consequences. Commercial claims are not efficacy evidence; unavailable books are not treated as read.
2. **Deep coaching methods.** Add diagnosis-to-intervention guidance, framework selection, an evidence/story inventory, question-specific preparation, full transcript analysis, feedback handling, and a bounded multi-session practice cycle. Include worked contrasts and observable transfer checks.
3. **Occupational depth.** Provide focused playbooks across at least twelve professional contexts. Each needs role-relevant interview tasks, evidence criteria, sample probes, a weak/strong contrast using synthetic facts, candidate drills, interviewer adaptations, and differences by responsibility level. Use authoritative role/process material and identify authored adaptations.
4. **Complete behavioral execution.** Execute all 18 existing output-quality scenarios, plus newly needed content-quality cases. Run complete multi-turn candidate coaching, simulation, and interviewer-design sessions. Save actual outputs and task instructions; report semantic review separately from deterministic checks. Model execution/review is not human adjudication. Repair observed defects and rerun affected cases.
5. **Review and delivery.** Check every original requirement against concrete artifacts; verify routing, source access/limits, progressive disclosure, source attribution, portability, and schema/packaging consistency. Keep main instructions below repository limits and each reference below 60,000 characters. Refresh generated files and run focused repository checks. Deliver local committed work and exact results; publication still requires the separately requested approval.

## Evidence and stopping rules

No outcome guarantee, profession-completeness claim, or validated hiring predictor is required or asserted. A short model output does not count as a completed mock. Track tests as executed, failed, repaired, unresolved, or not assessed; retain original outputs. Stop a non-converging diagnostic/fix thread after three passes and report the concrete issue. Do not buy books, access private course materials, contact practitioners, publish, or use personal/private transcripts without authorization. Public research and local implementation proceed within scope.
