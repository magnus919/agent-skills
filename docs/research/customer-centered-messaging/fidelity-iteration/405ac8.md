## 3. Next Action & Feasible Path to Value

### Primary Call to Action
**Request a summary walkthrough**

### Action Expectations
When you request a walkthrough, we review the sample dataset together to show how CSV tickets map into building status views and exported summaries.
*Open note:* Scheduling windows, follow-up timelines, and contact terms are currently unconfirmed and will be specified upon booking.

### Path from Evaluation to Production
1. **Review sample data:** Walk through the interface using the provided sample dataset to confirm that the exported fields meet reporting requirements.
2. **Agree on import specifications:** Coordinate between your team and our technical staff to match your ticket CSV column headers (building, ticket identifier, status) to the software’s import format.
3. **Assign operational responsibility:** Identify who on your property coordination team will prepare and submit ticket files.
4. **Begin ongoing reporting:** Import ticket files as needed and export dated status summaries for distribution.
*Prerequisite & timeline note:* Production use requires completing steps 2 and 3. Total onboarding duration is dependent on field alignment and is currently unquantified.

## 4. Resident-Representative Pitch

> **Summary for Resident Representatives:**
> Property coordinators can provide you with an exported summary showing the current recorded status of repair tickets across your building, dated at the time of export. This summary clarifies what is currently logged in property management records without requiring you to interpret raw spreadsheets or message threads.
>
> *Important scope limits:* The summary reflects status at export time. It does not provide direct account login, live notification feeds, digital signatures, independent auditor verification, or timestamped proof of physical repair completion.

## 5. Short Brand Card

```markdown
# Brand Card: Repair Window

### Master Promise
A plain, reliable utility for property coordinators to consolidate repair ticket status from CSV imports into dated building summaries.

### Positioning
For property coordinators managing repairs across multiple properties, Repair Window is a status-tracking utility that imports CSV tickets into a unified building view and produces clean, dated summary exports—replacing scattered emails and multi-tab spreadsheets.

### Core Attributes
- Exact: Claims describe literal software inputs, data fields, and outputs.
- Plain: Direct, unadorned vocabulary; no jargon, tech mysticism, or hype.
- Professional: Respectful, calm, and rigorous about data limitations.

### Deliverables & Mechanism
- Inputs: CSV ticket data files.
- Display: Building name and current ticket status.
- Exports: Summary file listing building, current status, and date of export.

### Explicit Limits & Unknowns
- Not supplied / Unknown: Resident accounts, sharing permissions, user-change audit logs, repair-completion timestamps, digital signatures, immutability, independent certifications, retention periods, cost/time savings.
```

## 7. Strategy & UX Handoff Notes

- **To Brand Designer:** Preserve the factual boundary in all sales collateral. Do not convert "dated export" into "tamper-proof record" or "real-time transparency." Ensure typography and collateral reflect a utilitarian, disciplined service.
- **To Product Design & UX:** The walkthrough request page must capture contact details without promising immediate automated scheduling until operational terms are defined. The sample-summary preview must visibly display the static export date and status fields to manage user expectations.
