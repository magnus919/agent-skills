## Customer-Facing Copy

### Homepage Opening

**Print-ready map layouts for Seabrook heritage groups**

Map Edition turns your group’s supplied images and captions into a print-ready map-layout PDF. You receive a written layout review before you decide whether to approve a separate print order.

### Sample-Page Caption

> **Sample map-layout page**
> An illustrative layout page demonstrating formatting and caption placement from supplied files. Detailed review methods, paper, ink, printing processes, and archival specifications are determined separately.

### Action Expectations & Short Path

**Main Action:** Request a layout-review appointment
*What happens next:* Submit your request, and Map Edition will follow up to confirm appointment availability. Submitting a request does not book an instant time slot or place a print order. An initial fee applies to the layout review.

#### How It Works
1. **Supply your material** — Gather your heritage group’s images and captions.
2. **Request an appointment** — Submit an appointment request for your layout review; availability is confirmed afterward.
3. **Receive your layout review** — Get a print-ready map-layout PDF and a written layout review for an initial review fee.
4. **Decide on printing** — Review the written feedback before choosing whether to approve a separate print order.

### Objection: “Am I ordering the full print run?”

**No.** Your layout review is a distinct first step. You receive a written layout review and a print-ready PDF so your group can evaluate the layout before committing to print production. Any print order is handled separately and requires your explicit approval. (A fee applies to the layout review.)
