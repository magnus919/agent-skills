## 3. Feasible Path & Primary Action

### How It Works
1. **Request an appointment:** Submit an appointment request for your fabric sample.
2. **Send your sample:** Once studio availability is confirmed, mail or deliver your fabric sample to our Harborside studio (the studio does not offer sample collection).
3. **Receive your assessment:** Review your written condition report and proceed-or-reconsider recommendation before deciding whether to approve a subsequent pattern project.

### Main Action
* **Action Label:** `Request an Assessment Appointment`
* **Expectation & Consequence Note:** Submit your request to begin scheduling. We will review studio capacity and contact you to confirm availability. A sample-assessment fee applies to each review. Requesting an appointment does not commit you to a future pattern project.

---

## 4. Objection Handling

**Question:** *Do I have to commission a pattern project first?*

**Answer:**
No. You do not need an active or scheduled pattern project to use this service. We review standalone fabric samples that you send to our studio. You receive a written condition assessment with a proceed-or-reconsider recommendation first, allowing you to evaluate the material before approving any future pattern work. A separate sample-assessment fee applies to this evaluation.
