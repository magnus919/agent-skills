## Frequently Asked Questions

### Am I ordering the full print run when I request a layout review?
**No.** Requesting a review books an appointment to evaluate your layout and create a print-ready PDF. It does not place or authorize a print run.

The layout review carries a separate fee (terms and amounts confirmed prior to booking). Once you receive the completed layout PDF and written review, your heritage group decides independently whether, when, and where to approve a separate print order.
