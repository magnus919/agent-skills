# Customer Message Brief: Roster Window

**Audience:** Neighborhood organization coordinators (primary) and venue partners (secondary).
**Current Alternative:** Copied spreadsheets and forwarded email threads.
**Established Voice:** Exact and approachable.
**Evidence State:** Fictional supplied brief; untested draft.
**Known Capabilities:** CSV roster import, shift-by-shift assignment views, dated record of assignment changes.
**Explicit Unknowns:** Actor-identity logging, retention duration, integrity controls, external certifications, integrations beyond CSV, customer-result metrics, availability SLA, and setup turnaround time. (Recorded as unknown, not confirmed absent).

## 3. Action Expectations & Setup Path

### Primary Action
* **Label:** `Request a roster walkthrough`
* **Immediate Expectation:** Demonstrates shift views and dated change tracking using a sample dataset. Scheduling, review format, and confirmation timelines will be provided upon inquiry. Requesting a walkthrough does not activate live software or initiate data migration.

### Production Setup Path
A feasible transition requires completing specific administrative prerequisites:
1. **Walkthrough & Scope Check:** Review sample views to confirm shift coordination fit.
2. **Data & Ownership Alignment:** Agree on standard CSV column headers and designate organizational owners responsible for uploading rosters and managing updates.
3. **Initial Import:** Import the confirmed CSV roster and begin tracking shift assignments and dated edits.
*(Note: Setup timeline depends on your team's readiness to define fields and assign responsibilities; no guaranteed launch duration is established).*

## 6. Before / After Revision Record

### Legacy Copy Diagnostic

| Legacy Passage | Defect Diagnosis | Evidence & Boundary Context |
|---|---|---|
| *"The certified, tamper-proof roster..."* | Claims third-party compliance certification and immutable data controls. | **Unsupported.** Certifications and integrity controls are unknown. |
| *"...that guarantees every volunteer turns up."* | Overpromises an uncontrollable human behavioral outcome. Software cannot prevent volunteer absences. | **Unsupported.** Only displays assignments and logs edits. Attendance guarantees must be removed. |
| *"Get instant access."* | Conceals onboarding requirements. Production use requires field mapping and operational ownership. | **Misleading path.** First step is an evaluation walkthrough; live setup requires prerequisite preparation. |
