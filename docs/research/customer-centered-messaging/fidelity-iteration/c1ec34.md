# Customer Message Brief & Brand Handoff: Roster Window

**Status:** Untested draft
**Tone/Voice:** Exact and approachable
**Target Audience:** Neighborhood volunteer coordinators (primary), venue partners (stakeholder)
**Alternative:** Copied spreadsheets and email threads

---

## 1. Homepage Opening

### Headline
**Coordinate neighborhood volunteer shifts without juggling spreadsheets**

### Supporting Line
Roster Window lets you import a CSV roster, view assignments by shift, and maintain a dated record of assignment changes in one central view.

---

## 2. Proof Caption

> **Caption for Walkthrough Interface Preview:**
> *Sample walkthrough view showing volunteer assignments grouped by shift alongside a chronological, dated log of updates. (Sample data shown for illustration. The record logs change dates; it does not log editor identity or guarantee volunteer attendance.)*

---

## 3. Action Expectations & Setup Path

### Primary Call to Action
**Request a roster walkthrough**

* **What happens next:** Walk through shift views and dated change tracking using a sample dataset. *(Scheduling and response turnaround terms are currently unassigned and will be confirmed after request submission.)*
* **What it is not:** This is an exploratory walkthrough, not an instant account provisioning or a live data import.

### Setup Path to Value
1. **Sample Walkthrough:** Tour shift views and inspect how dated change records operate using sample volunteer data.
2. **Field & Role Agreement:** Align on required CSV import fields and agree on coordinator administration responsibilities. *(Prerequisite for setup; completion duration depends on organizational scope.)*
3. **CSV Import & Shift Setup:** Import your existing roster via CSV and publish shift assignments for coordinators and venue teams.

---

## 4. Venue-Partner Pitch Opener

> “To make shift handoffs straightforward, Roster Window gives venue partners a clear view of volunteer coverage alongside a dated record of every schedule change. You can see current coverage and verify when an assignment was last updated without sorting through forwarded email threads.”
