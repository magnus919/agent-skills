## 1. Decision Context & Semantic Anchors

* **Target Audience:** Greenhouse operators and facility managers managing meter data across multiple growing environments; finance reviewers evaluating records for billing and expense substantiation.
* **Current Alternative:** Maintaining separate spreadsheets per meter or zone, aggregated manually into monthly email summaries.
* **Core Offer:** A focused application to upload CSV meter readings, review them consolidated by room and day, and export a dated summary of submitted readings.
* **Prerequisites for Production:** Agreeing on CSV import fields and confirming internal ownership of file submissions.
* **Available Proof:** A sample dataset demonstrated in a guided walkthrough.
* **Status of Non-evidenced Capabilities (Unknowns):** Live sensor telemetry, cryptographic/signed records, immutability, third-party validation, formal certifications, historical cost savings, carbon accounting, automated integrations beyond CSV, data-retention schedules, and support SLAs are unconfirmed inputs. They are neither claimed as features nor asserted as confirmed absences in customer-facing copy.

## 3. Main Action & Setup Path

### Call to Action
* **Primary Button:** `Request a sample-data walkthrough`
* **Action Expectation & Terms:** Submit facility details to schedule an introductory walkthrough using sample greenhouse data. *(Scheduling availability and follow-up response timelines are confirmed upon contact; no automated booking window is promised.)*

### Production Setup Path
A clear three-stage path from initial review to operational use:
1. **Sample Walkthrough:** Inspect room views, daily aggregation, and the dated summary export using sample data.
2. **Field & Workflow Setup:** Define and align required CSV column formats (meter ID, timestamp, reading, room identifier) and assign responsible team members for file uploads.
3. **Operational Submissions:** Begin uploading regular CSV files to generate consolidated daily views and export dated summaries.

## 4. Finance-Reviewer Pitch

> **For Finance & Billing Reviewers:**
> “Room Readings provides a centralized, dated record of all CSV meter files submitted across your greenhouse facilities. Instead of reconciling disparate spreadsheets attached to email threads, finance teams can export a unified summary of recorded usage organized by room and calendar day to substantiate internal allocations and utility billings against submitted operational inputs.”

* **What it substantiates:** Matches submitted CSV readings to specific rooms and dates within a single exported ledger.
* **Boundary limits:** Reflects submitted CSV data as uploaded; does not provide independent meter certification, tamper-evident hardware sealing, or automated utility tariff verification.
