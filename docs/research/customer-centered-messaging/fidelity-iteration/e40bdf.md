## 3. Path and Main Action

### How It Works
1. **Request an appointment:** Submit your appointment request to review your fabric samples. Studio availability is confirmed after you submit.
2. **Send your samples:** Mail or deliver your fabric samples directly to our Harborside studio. (Studio pickup is not available.)
3. **Receive your assessment:** Review your written fabric-condition assessment and recommendation before deciding whether to commission a pattern project.

### Main Action
*Button label:* **Request a sample-assessment appointment**

*Direct expectation note:*
> An assessment fee applies to sample reviews. Submitting this request does not confirm a booking; our studio will follow up to confirm appointment availability. Assessment fee amount, payment timing, and terms are confirmed prior to booking.

## 4. Objection Answer

**Question:** *Do I have to commission a pattern project first?*

**Answer:**
No. Loom Review assesses your fabric samples before you commit to a pattern project. You send your samples to our Harborside studio, pay a sample-assessment fee, and receive a written condition assessment with a clear proceed-or-reconsider recommendation. You can then make an informed decision before approving subsequent pattern work.
