## 5. Brand Card

```markdown
# Brand Card: Repair Window

## Overview
- Category: Property repair ticket aggregation and summary software.
- Primary Audience: Property coordinators managing repairs across multiple properties.
- Secondary Audience: Resident representatives reviewing building repair status summaries.
- Core Offer: Import CSV repair tickets, view status by building, and export dated summaries.

## Core Positioning
For property coordinators relying on separate spreadsheets and email threads,
Repair Window provides a centralized display of building repair statuses and
dated summary exports derived directly from imported CSV files.

## Tone & Style
- Exact: State concrete software inputs and outputs without exaggeration.
- Plain: Use direct operational language; avoid tech jargon or ungrounded guarantees.
- Professional: Respectful, measured, and matter-of-fact.

## Messaging Anchors & Boundaries
- Capabilities Evidenced: CSV import, building display, current ticket status, dated summary export.
- Bound Limits: Export date reflects generation time, not individual task completion timestamps.
- Explicit Unknowns: Resident direct-access accounts, sharing permissions, actor-identity audit logging, retention limits, third-party certification, and quantified labor savings are unknown. Do not assert presence or absence.
```
