## 3. Next-Action Expectations and Steps

### Call to Action
**Request a site-assessment visit**

### What Happens Next
Submitting a request does not book an assessment date or commit your museum to a project. We review local scheduling and confirm our current capacity and visit dates with you afterward.

### Prerequisites and Path to Value
1. **Request an initial site assessment**
   Submit your museum’s location in Pinehaven and collection details. We confirm visit availability and scheduling.
2. **Complete the on-site assessment**
   An initial site-assessment fee applies. To complete the assessment, your team must provide access to the collection areas and your existing shelf labels.
3. **Review project scope and proposal**
   Following the assessment, you receive a proposal detailing the scope for a full inventory project. You decide whether to commission the separate project; the assessment does not automatically start or schedule full inventory work.
4. **Receive your photographic inventory**
   If approved, we photograph objects across designated shelves, link the images to your supplied shelf identifiers, and deliver the final photographic records alongside a written list of unmatched items.

*(Note for project owner: Assessment fee amount, payment timing, refund terms, transport arrangements, and turnaround times remain open decisions to be defined before contract execution.)*

---

## 4. Objection Handling

### “Am I agreeing to the full inventory project?”

**No.** Requesting a visit begins only the initial site assessment, which carries its own assessment fee.

The site assessment allows us to examine your space, collection access, and shelf labeling so we can define the scope of work. Once the assessment is complete, your board or staff can review the scope and decide whether to approve a separate inventory project. An assessment visit does not obligate you to proceed with full cataloging.

## 6. Revision & Fact-Check Record

### Diagnosis of Old Copy
* **Supplied Old Copy:** *“Museum-grade certainty before you spend a cent. Accredited experts protect every object. Book instantly.”*
* **Defects Identified:**
  1. *“Before you spend a cent”* directly contradicts the business model; an initial site-assessment fee is mandatory before any project decision is made.
  2. *“Accredited experts protect every object”* introduces unsupported claims of professional accreditation, preservation, physical protection, and handling techniques. Accreditation and preservation services are unevidenced unknowns, not established capabilities.
  3. *“Book instantly”* misrepresents the intake process; capacity and dates are subject to later confirmation, not immediate automated booking.
  4. *“Museum-grade certainty”* is ungrounded marketing hyperbole that obscures the tangible deliverable (linked shelf photos and an unmatched items list).
