### 3. Action Expectations and Path to Value

**Primary Action Label:**
`Request a layout-review appointment`

**What Happens After Clicking:**
Submitting a request sends your details to our team. We will review our schedule and follow up to confirm appointment availability. Submitting a request does not book an instant calendar slot, does not initiate billing, and does not place a print order.

**Path to Value:**

1. **Request an appointment:** Submit your group’s project details. We will contact you to confirm appointment availability.
2. **Supply your materials:** Provide your group’s images and captions to be assembled into a print-ready PDF layout.
3. **Receive your written layout review:** Examine the completed layout PDF alongside our written review (layout review fee applies).
4. **Decide on printing:** Use the finalized PDF with your chosen printer, or evaluate and approve a separate print order.

---

### 4. Objection & Clarity: “Am I ordering the full print run?”

> **Question: Am I ordering the full print run?**
> **Answer:** No. Requesting an appointment and receiving a written layout review does not order or commit you to a print run. The layout review is an independent step with its own fee, designed to check your print-ready map PDF before any press commitment. You review and approve a separate print order only if and when you decide to proceed with printing.
