## 3. Path to Value & Main Action

### How It Works

1. **Request an assessment appointment:** Submit an initial request for your fabric sample. Availability is confirmed following submission.
2. **Send your fabric sample:** Ship or deliver your sample material directly to our Harborside studio (studio collection is not available).
3. **Receive your written assessment:** An assessment fee applies prior to or as part of the evaluation (fee amount, schedule, and credit terms to be confirmed). You receive a written condition review and a clear proceed-or-reconsider recommendation before deciding on subsequent pattern work.

### Main Call to Action

- **Button / Action Label:** Request Assessment Appointment
- **Action Expectations:** Submitting a request reserves a review slot subject to studio capacity. It does not commit you to a pattern project or guarantee immediate booking. Staff will confirm availability and communicate applicable assessment fee terms directly; no guaranteed response time is established.

## 4. Objection Handling: Pattern Project Dependency

**Question:** *Do I have to commission a pattern project first?*

**Answer:**
No. Loom Review evaluates your fabric sample independently before you approve or fund any pattern work. You receive a standalone written assessment and recommendation so you can evaluate the material first. (Note: A dedicated sample-assessment fee applies to this evaluation; exact fee amounts, billing timing, and whether this fee applies toward future services are confirmed upon appointment request.)
