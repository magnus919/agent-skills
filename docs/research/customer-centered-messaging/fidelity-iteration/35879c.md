### 3. Next-Action Expectations and Steps to Value

**Primary action label:**
`Request a site-assessment visit`

**What happens after requesting:**
Submitting a request sends your museum's details to our team. We will review our schedule and contact you to confirm available dates and assessment capacity. Requesting a visit does not schedule an appointment automatically, nor does it commit your board or staff to an inventory project.

#### Feasible Steps to Value

1. **Request a site-assessment visit:** Submit your collection location in Pinehaven and contact details. We confirm scheduling availability and capacity directly with you.
2. **Complete the initial site assessment:** We conduct an on-site evaluation of the collection space to determine scope. *(Note: An initial site-assessment fee applies before any further commitment. Specific fee amount, payment due dates, refund terms, and potential credit toward subsequent work are confirmed during scheduling.)*
3. **Decide on the inventory project:** Based on the assessment, your museum decides whether to approve a separate, full inventory project.
4. **Prepare collection access:** If approved, your staff provides physical access to the collection areas and ensures existing shelf labels are in place. *(Handling procedures, transport requirements, insurance coverage, and completion turnaround times are established in project scoping.)*
5. **Receive inventory deliverables:** Shelf Map delivers your catalog of object photographs linked to your shelf identifiers, accompanied by a written record of any unmatched items.

---

### 4. Frequently Asked Question

**Am I agreeing to the full inventory project by booking an assessment?**

No. The site assessment and the inventory project are two distinct stages:

* The **site-assessment visit** allows us to examine the physical shelving and collection layout to determine project feasibility. An initial site-assessment fee applies for this visit.
* The **inventory project** is an entirely separate engagement. You review the findings and scope after the assessment before deciding whether to commission the photographic inventory. Requesting or completing an assessment places your museum under no obligation to proceed with the larger project.

*(Note: Invoicing schedules, fee structures, cancellation or refund policies, and whether the assessment fee applies toward subsequent project costs will be provided before confirming your assessment visit.)*
