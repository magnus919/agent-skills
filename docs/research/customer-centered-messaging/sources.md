## S1 — Official introduction

- URL: https://storybrand.com/downloads/intro-to-sb/Introduction-to-StoryBrand.pdf
- Retrieved title: *Your Brand Is Not the Hero*, Donald Miller. Publication date not established.
- Retrieval: `scrape` failed HTTP 502; `download --extract-text` succeeded (15+ printed pages, 13,679 extracted characters).
- Quality: authoritative description of the publisher's method; promotional, not independent effectiveness evidence.
- C1, pp. 7–8: narrative components cover a person with a goal, an obstacle, assistance, a plan, action, and favorable or unfavorable consequences.
- C2, pp. 7, 12–13: the customer occupies the central role; the business supplies help. Credibility combines understanding the customer's difficulty with demonstrated capability.
- C3, p. 7: the method distinguishes practical difficulties, feelings, and broader values. This is a framework distinction; it does not establish that every customer has all three.
- C4, pp. 8, 13: action should be explicit and the outcome intelligible; negative consequences provide stakes. The publisher itself cautions against fear mongering in its insurance example.
- C5, pp. 4, 15–16: revenue anecdotes and testimonials lack controls or attribution analysis in this document. Its claims about brains, movies, and election results do not supply causal evidence.
- Extraction disposition: all decision-relevant components, operational implications, and evidence limitations represented above. Film plots, promotional invitations, and testimonial wording omitted as unnecessary.

## S2 — Official website guide

- URL: https://storybrand.com/downloads/5-Things-Your-Website-Should-Include.pdf
- Identification: five website recommendations, Donald Miller; publication date not established.
- Retrieval: `scrape` failed HTTP 502; `download --extract-text` succeeded (12,068 characters).
- Quality: primary implementation advice with anecdotes; no controlled website conversion evaluation supplied.
- C6, sections 1–2, pp. 4–8: explain the offer plainly and make a relevant purchase or consultation action easy to find.
- C7, section 3, p. 10: imagery should help visitors envision the benefit. Its categorical claim that purchases relieve internal tension exceeds the evidence shown.
- C8, section 4, pp. 12–13: organize a broad offering into comprehensible groups. Exact category counts and revenue-first placement are prescriptions, not demonstrated universal optima.
- C9, section 5, pp. 15–16: maintain consistent answers about audience needs, difficulty, understanding, capability, process, action, and outcome across channels.
- C10, introduction: a five-second comprehension rule and sales-improvement promises are presented without a testing protocol. Retain rapid comprehension as a testable objective, not a law.
- Extraction disposition: five recommendations and their material limitations extracted. Testimonials and workshop sales copy excluded.

## S6 — Independent web-writing research

- URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/
- Title: *Concise, SCANNABLE, and Objective: How to Write for the Web*, Morkes and Nielsen.
- Retrieval: `scrape` succeeded. Studies conducted in summer 1997; source is historical, not current device/market research.
- Quality: original empirical report with participants, tasks, comparison conditions and measures. Independent of StoryBrand; investigates only adjacent writing principles.
- C14, Study 3: 51 users compared five versions of a small travel-information site. Clear structure and restrained prose improved measured usability in this setting.
- C15, results and normalization: reported 124% improvement is a composite usability comparison, not purchases, revenue, a StoryBrand treatment, or an expected effect size for a new site.
- C16, future research: task/site generalization and interactions between writing dimensions require further study.
- Disposition: retained empirical support for testing clarity, scanning and factual writing, plus transfer limitations. No inference that the seven-part sequence has been independently validated.
