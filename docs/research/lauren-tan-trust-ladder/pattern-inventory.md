# Exportable patterns from Lauren Tan and Matt Pocock

The catalog’s recorded principles govern this comparison. An **Enrich** entry identifies a missing practical seam or an underrepresented example in an existing skill. **Trial** means the technique is plausible, but warrants a representative comparison before changing guidance. **Covered** means the current catalog has the substance. Its example can be exercised in practice. **Keep local** marks an operating choice that depends on Tan’s team, tools, or policy.

I extracted patterns from the complete caption track in chronological order, then split ideas that require different evidence or authority. I checked the closest catalog owner for each and inspected public implementations or instructions where available. GitHub entries tagged **companion** extend a topic raised in the interview. They establish published design, and some include actual implementation. They provide no measurement of field outcomes. Times below refer to the supplied cut. The [source and provenance record](README.md#evidence-and-limits) gives the inspected catalog revision and caption coverage.

## Source choices that stay with their local setting

- **Keep local** · Dune’s specific Electron conventions, Cursor Projects, Grok Bot connector wiring, slash commands, transcript directories, and Tan’s named principles. They disclose implementation and style choices. The portable behaviors above have existing catalog owners.
- **Keep local** · A fixed number or mix of reviewer models, a universal preference for post-merge review, blanket build restrictions, and a team’s delegation ceilings. The catalog sets those decisions by local risk, observed effectiveness, environment and policy.
- **Keep local** · PR volume as a target, predictions about specific formal verification languages, and universal extrapolation from one team’s throughput. The evidence supports considering the working practices and testing their applicability.

The [worked autonomy decision](README.md#a-worked-promotion-decision-to-test-first) illustrates one candidate. Proposed enrichments use the existing owner’s principle, template or method as the starting point.
