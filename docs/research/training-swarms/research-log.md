## Source records

| Source and evidence type | Extracted claims and limitations | Disposition and destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hugging Face, Training Swarms](https://huggingface.co/blog/training-swarms), Nov 26, 2026. First-party experiment and tutorial. | Four agents share Qwen3-0.6B weights and have separate contexts and private integers. Correctness is team-level and repeats of equal values must be preserved. Eight independent team attempts per problem yield a team-relative training advantage. Token penalty is applied to successful teams. First-to-last five-update means were 6.56% to 82.34%, with generated tokens decreasing from 12,826.9 to 8,026.0 and broadcasts increasing from 2.38 to 5.73. Final held-out result was 26/32. No held-out efficiency observations were preserved. The early-window best-of-eight estimate of 37.5% versus late one-attempt 82.3% uses training problems, with partial training already present in early updates. It is an approximate comparison, and withholding required inputs prevents a meaningful solo scale-up comparison. | Retain for the private-information comparison, accounting limits and diagnostics. Numbers are single-source within this specific run. |
| [Jha et al., s³ v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.08615v1), Nov 5, 2026. Controlled preprint, §3–7 and appendices I, J, K and M. | Shared team reward, asynchronous delivery, separate contexts and one trained policy. Four-agent aggregation reached about 93% after 200 updates, versus 35% online imitation and 13% individual reward. Individual reward performed comparably to shared reward for exploration. Exploration without communication lost 19 points at four agents, and room overlap was nearly halved in the communicating condition. Shared corridors contribute to that overlap metric. The four-agent comparison with solo-trained best-of-four failed the corrected significance test. At six the gain was about seven points and survived correction. At sixteen all aggregation arms struggled. Training traces include early sharing, fewer repeated broadcasts at convergence, failed synthesis with complete information and undue trust in reports. Generated-token limits and use are narrower than end-to-end system cost. Two synthetic tasks, one training run per condition, with held-out evaluations repeated across six seeds. | Retain as primary experimental-design evidence. Translate comparisons, information dependencies and diagnostics into the brief and application. Keep numeric outcomes workload-specific. |
| [Park et al., Scaling Discovery through Test-Time Communication](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21032), 2026. Fixed-policy controlled preprint, §3–4. | Compares communicating peers, independent sampling and extended solo search. Benefits on selected discovery tasks depend on informative intermediate feedback and enough per-agent budget. Lower budgets erased the advantage in reported conditions. On Terminal-Bench 2.0, limited trials yielded mean 60.67% for the communicating pair and 62.36% for independent pass@2. The run does not establish a stable effect size or cause there. Shared state, homogeneous workers and task selection limit transfer. The authors identify herding as a failure mode. | Retain for actionable intermediate progress and adverse cases in the brief and application. |
| [Anthropic, Patterns and Problems in Multiagent Systems](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems), 2026. First-party experimental report. | The comparison changes both resource use and reachable work. Restricting the reported outputs to a common search surface gives roughly comparable token efficiency for the model discussed. In a separate game-building experiment, visible activity and some successful integration coexisted with poor end products. The study also describes conformity. | Retain the scope control and independent product acceptance lessons. Experimental mechanics from the security study are outside the application. |
| [Anthropic, How We Built Our Multi-Agent Research System](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multi-agent-research-system), 2025. Reported internal gains vary model mix, and the report identifies token use as a major explanatory variable in a related evaluation. The hierarchy illustrates an option. Its results do not isolate communication or establish transfer to tightly coupled work. | Retain as catalog context. The existing skill already uses this source for delegation and cost cautions. |
