## Coverage map

- **Adopt/scale/constrain/redesign/retire:** entry modes and structured dispositions
  connect outcome, comparison, full cost, guardrails, owner and reversal.
- **Productivity versus value:** time saved, capacity released and realized economic
  benefit are distinct. Displaced work, review/correction, exception handling and
  concurrent changes are explicit, not hidden in a token-only denominator.
- **Causal limits:** evidence method chooses the strongest feasible design and narrows
  the claim when only descriptive or vendor evidence exists. Statistical analysis
  stays with data-scientist, detailed metrics with product analytics.
- **Heterogeneity/countermetrics:** worker experience, task risk, customer segment,
  quality, safety and shifted burden can reverse the aggregate recommendation.
- **Uncertainty/ranges:** source classes, scenario/sensitivity fields, unknowns and
  owners constrain decision language; numeric precision cannot fill missing costs.
- **Authority/reversibility:** governance packet, next bounded population/action slice,
  external enforcement, expiry/revalidation, containment and exit link economics to
  governance/runtime owners without allowing a positive business case to grant access.
- **Supervision:** human review, escalation, worker burden and full control cost are
  already represented. #762 tests whether a common capacity worksheet adds a
  repeated new coordination value; this audit supplies no such validation.
- **Trigger behavior:** description explicitly owns workflow value and pilot scale/
  constrain/retire decisions. No client activation was executed; the source coverage
  cannot establish an activation rate or measure routing improvement.
