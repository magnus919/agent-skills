# Five synthesized solo-operator walkthroughs

**All cases are fictional.** This package answers the user's request to synthesize
an exercise when actual-user records were unavailable. It is useful for reviewing
handoff coherence, economic arithmetic and failure boundaries. It does not satisfy
#758's five-consented-user requirement, establish independent repeated failures,
validate orchestration value, or authorize a new bundle.

Catalog pin: `d64c1e79dbd4d9bf7c2ca092cd950995d96c7b82`.
`protocol.md` defines the screen; `cases.json` holds invented inputs and route-only
recommendations; `handoff-record.md` is the minimal record; `handoffs.json` applies
it to each case; `screen.json` records computed arithmetic and compares the authored
dispositions. No private business documents or conversations were used.

| Fictional operator | Decision-bearing contradiction | Modeled economics | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reporting consultant | Refundable deposit for a manual pilot is treated as a reason to build software | $1,200 price − $700 delivery cost = $500 contribution; two pilots recover $1,000 setup; two fit stated monthly capacity | Proceed with one bounded manual pilot; defer platform |
| Course creator | Compliments are treated as sales; unlimited feedback makes scope unclear | Course needs 23 paid seats at $90 contribution; alternate workshop needs four paid seats at $120 contribution against $480 fixed cost | Revise to workshop presale hypothesis; defer course |

Five standard orders leave $65 modeled setup loss within the invented $100 learning cap | Reject custom scope at old price; bounded standard-product test only |
| Freelance translator | Quality-review time is omitted and a quote request is called retention | Actual-in-the-fiction six hours plus tools cost $260 against $300 revenue; contribution $40; four jobs recover $160 setup; one fits remaining capacity | Hold expansion; inspect one further same-scope job if authorized |

These are partial economic models, not complete business valuations. Taxes,
uncertainty, acquisition, refunds and opportunity cost would need appropriate
inputs before real decisions; omitted categories are not assumed zero in real use.
The arithmetic applies only to the explicitly invented cost boundary. A profitable
unit contribution does not establish demand, quality, cash safety or a viable firm.

## What the record makes inspectable

Each handoff names the current promise, evidence class, cost/capacity assumptions,
contradiction, next specialist, and stop/revision trigger. In S4, a promise change
invalidates the old economics. In S5, delivery evidence supersedes the estimate.
S1 preserves refundable versus retained payment; S2 keeps preference separate from
paid validation; S3 preserves a stop despite favorable payment evidence.

The competent route-only recommendations and the record-based recommendations are
the same in this authored exercise. The record organizes their provenance; there
is no demonstrated incremental decision benefit. Neither that equality nor five
constructed contradictions measures actual model behavior or human usefulness.

the record is a research aid, not another methodology or installed skill. There is
no basis to claim three independent users experienced a common failure or that the
record materially improved a real decision.
