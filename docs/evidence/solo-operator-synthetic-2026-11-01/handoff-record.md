# Minimal cross-specialist handoff record

Research template. Fill with source IDs and evidence status; a blank is unknown,
not permission to invent evidence. This record does not approve contact or spend.

1. Decision, operator/owner, business type, next commitment and review trigger.
2. Customer/problem claim, evidence IDs, date and class (preference, reported
   behavior, observation, commitment, payment, delivery, repeat purchase).
3. What that evidence supports and what remains untested.
4. Current bounded offer, exclusions, customer dependencies and acceptance criteria.
5. Price, variable cost including operator labor, fixed/setup cost, capacity,
   assumptions, uncertainty and cash/refund exposure.
6. Contradictions or changed scope; earlier claim/price/decision superseded and
   which downstream records must be rechecked.
7. Smallest next test, exposure/time/cost cap, meaningful commitment, and owner.
8. Continue/revise/stop/inconclusive criteria and reversal/exit path.
9. Delivery/rework/acceptance and retention evidence, if it actually exists.
10. Next specialist, exact question to answer, and evidence passed to it.

For this synthetic screen every evidence entry remains fictional. In real use,
consent, redaction and retention boundaries must be recorded with actual cases.
