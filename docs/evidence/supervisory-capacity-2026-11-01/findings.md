# Supervisory-capacity validation — #762

**No-build recommendation for this evidence.** One common worksheet was applied
to three privacy-preserving public reconstructions with different operating models.
It organized questions, but did not materially change the route-only recommendation
in any case. Public missing data does not establish a repeated real failure.
No bundle, skill, employee score or surveillance mechanism is added.

## Method and limits

Catalog pinned at `d284993fc6dc786d53496a3e9a7298de87292715`.
`protocol.md` fixes the go/no-go rule and source limits. `baseline.json` preserves
route-only recommendations before filling `worksheet.md`; `cases.md` records the
source facts, filled dimensions, distinct constraints, proposed dispositions and
comparison. These are faithfully source-bounded historical/public contexts, not
consented participants, independently observed workflows, a blinded trial or actual
organizational decisions changed by this exercise.

Sources are first-party reports/policies. Direct observation is limited to the
published text. Company metrics remain attributed reports; all recommendations and
interpretations are analyst inference. Unknown private facts stay unknown rather
than being turned into claims of inadequate real staffing or governance.

These owners already cover the consequential decisions. A table containing fields
from each is not by itself a demonstrated new orchestration capability.

## Case and gate result

Three cases were assessed: customer service, software DevEx, and research/engineering.
At least S1 explicitly recommends constraining proposed unattended consequential
scope; S2 retains human review rather than bypassing it to absorb generated output.
Capacity, competence, authority and attention are recorded separately throughout.

| Gate | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Same decision failure in two independent cases | Not established | Public reports leave workload and review details unknown; absence from a source is not an observed organizational failure |
| Existing routing insufficient | Not demonstrated | All proposed actions route to current owners in the table above |
| Worksheet materially changes the decision | Not demonstrated | Three authored baseline/worksheet comparisons retain the same consequential disposition; no actual organization decision change was measured |

The conjunctive go gate does not pass. **Do not recommend a new bundle or a
follow-on implementation from these cases.** This is not proof that a worksheet
could never help another organization, nor a claim of validated human benefit.

## Next evidence and acceptance boundary

Keep the worksheet as a bounded research artifact. If real, authorized decision
records later show the same lost handoff or supervision decision in two independent
organizations despite existing routing, test the smallest artifact on that exact
failure. Preserve the before/after decision, owner, consequence and disconfirming
case; require a material staffing/authority/sampling/escalation/instrumentation/
workflow/scope difference before proposing implementation. This does not ask for
private employee monitoring or a universal productivity score.

No private data, participant contact, staffing mutation, grant, deployment or new
skill was performed. Public source selection is a strong limitation; staffing
capacity, competence and real control effectiveness cannot be inferred from the
three organizations' output metrics. The raw source links and dates in `cases.md`
permit review without publishing copyrighted source copies or private records.
