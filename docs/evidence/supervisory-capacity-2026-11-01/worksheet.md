formula. Record source/date and evidence class for every entry; unknown is valid.

Capacity ≠ competence ≠ authority ≠ attention. Record each independently:
- Capacity: how much qualified review can actually be delivered in the window?
- Competence: who can judge this task and recognize the consequential error?
- Authority: who may decide, approve, refuse or stop the action?
- Attention: will the right evidence be noticed in time amid competing work?
