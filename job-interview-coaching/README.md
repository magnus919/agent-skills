# Job Interview Coaching

Prepare better answers, practise realistic interviews, and conduct interviews that reveal useful evidence.

## Why Install This Skill

Interview advice often stops at “use STAR” or a list of common questions. This skill helps your agent explain exactly where an answer succeeds or falls short, connect feedback to transcript evidence, and turn that feedback into focused practice. It works with first-job examples as well as experienced professional and leadership roles.

For hiring teams, it helps build relevant questions, neutral follow-ups, clear scoring criteria, and a better candidate experience. Guidance adapts to technical problems, business cases, portfolios, practical demonstrations, and behavioral interviews. Sixteen focused profession guides include worked contrasts, questions, drills, and differences from apprentice to executive level. Specialist standards still need appropriate sources and human judgment.

## Quick Start

No installation commands or API keys are required beyond installing the skill in your agent. Try:

> Review this interview transcript for a maintenance technician role. Show evidence for your three highest-priority improvements, then practise one with me.

Or:

> Help me design a 40-minute interview for an entry-level customer-service role, including neutral probes and scoring anchors.

## Triggers

- Preparing for a job interview or practising answers.
- Critiquing an interview transcript.
- Running a mock interview or a focused improvement drill.
- Designing hiring interviews or improving interviewer technique.
- Reviewing evidence against a job-related scorecard.

## Requirements

A conversational agent; no bundled executable scripts. A job description, interview invitation, or transcript improves specificity but is optional for starting practice. Current official sources and domain review are needed for specialist standards or jurisdiction-specific advice. Scores support practice and human review; they are not validated hiring predictions.
