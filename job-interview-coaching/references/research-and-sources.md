## Evidence hierarchy for continued work

Use actual role materials to establish the local task. Use professional standards and authoritative guidance for specialist content, research studies for empirical claims, and official employer pages for that employer's process. Books, coach blogs, commercial question banks, and candidate reports may provide useful hypotheses; label their perspective and check claims independently.

Do not reproduce copyrighted question banks or professional manuals. Create original practice questions and paraphrase supported principles with attribution. No proprietary coach curriculum was reviewed or reproduced in this version.

## What remains to research

- Full primary studies and newer syntheses on coaching effectiveness: intervention components, population, comparator, outcome, selection bias, effect persistence, and transfer to actual work. Avoid treating general workplace coaching meta-analyses as interview-specific proof.
- Practising coaches' documented methods and reference materials, including how they diagnose problems, adapt feedback, and measure transfer. ICF describes general coaching, not the complete practice of an interview coach.
- Profession-specific review of practical assessments in trades, transport, frontline service, finance, legal practice, teaching, and creative work. Existing packs provide useful prompts but not complete professional coverage.
- Cross-cultural and multilingual interviewing, disability and neurodivergence research, and differences between communication style and job performance. Avoid universal presentation norms.
- Candidate experience measures, interviewer-training studies, and work-sample validity. Keep applicant reactions separate from predictive validity.
- Current jurisdiction-specific hiring, recording, privacy, retention, accessibility, and automated-assessment requirements when deployment makes those relevant.

## Validation claims

A schema-valid eval manifest demonstrates only a well-formed scenario contract. Structural checks establish links, format, and packaging. Model-run samples can reveal failures but do not establish real-world hiring effectiveness. Independent domain review and representative candidate/interviewer use are still needed before describing the skill as world-class or validated.
