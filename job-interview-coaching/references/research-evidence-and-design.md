# Research evidence and design consequences

Read when assessing the basis of the methods or extending the skill. Checked 2026-10-23. This is a targeted evidence review, not a systematic review. Public practitioner methods, empirical studies, and this skill's authored procedures have different evidential status.

## Studies actually examined

**Daryanto et al., Conversate, full public HTML (2024 manuscript / published work linked on page).** Qualitative exploratory study with 19 participants. Adaptive questions, reflection on transcript segments, and dialogue about feedback were useful to participants. Reported difficulties included agreeable feedback, low-stakes realism, and frustration from repeated revision. The authors explicitly did not measure efficacy quantitatively. Design consequence: permit challenges to feedback, use bounded retries, and test new tasks. This supports design hypotheses, not hiring-outcome claims. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05570v2).

**Williams, 2012 dissertation, institutional abstract examined.** An experiment compared control, practice, and practice plus counselor feedback, with subsequent mock interviews rated by employer representatives. Hypotheses were only partially supported. Full PDF retrieval failed in this pass. Design consequence: measure independent performance separately from receiving feedback; do not assert that adding more feedback always improves results. Sample details and effect estimates remain unverified. [Institutional record](https://open.clemson.edu/all_dissertations/1009/).

**Travers and Huang, Applied Linguistics, 2021, publisher abstract examined.** A study of 27 high-proficiency additional-language candidates and nine professional interviewers compared control and two feedback/training conditions. The abstract reports higher interview ratings in the intervention groups and substantial influence of communication on judgments. Full text is purchase-restricted. Design consequence: distinguish communication from job capability and include interviewer awareness; do not claim language conformity or generalize to every language/context. [Publisher abstract](https://academic.oup.com/applij/article-abstract/42/4/641/6007975).

**Sackett, Zhang, Berry and Lievens, 2022, author institutional abstract examined.** Reanalysis identifies overcorrection for range restriction in prior selection-validity estimates; structured interviews remain useful but older magnitudes should not be repeated uncritically. Accepted manuscript download was inaccessible. Design consequence: no universal validity coefficient, no conversion of an interview rating into a hiring probability, and no claim that this locally authored rubric is validated. [Author institution](https://ink.library.smu.edu.sg/lkcsb_research/6894/).

**Levashina et al., 2014, publisher abstract and author-hosted discovery text examined.** Covers multiple elements of structure, including questions, rating, probes, bias, and reactions. Author PDF retrieval failed. Design consequence: structure administration and evaluation, not only the question list. Detailed quantitative findings are not reproduced. [Publisher record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/peps.12052).

**Maras et al., 2021, full open-access article and accepted manuscript examined.** Fifty adults (25 autistic, 25 non-autistic) completed baseline mock interviews; adapted-question interviews followed roughly six months later. Responses were rated from group-blinded transcripts. Adaptations included contextualized, separately sequenced questions, written questions, and clearer instructions. Ratings improved, particularly for autistic participants. Limits include mock conditions without a specific job description, transcript-based impressions, and a bundled sequential design that does not isolate each adjustment. Design consequence: make questions understandable and offer task support without treating this as proof of employment outcomes or a universal accommodation. [Full article](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1362361320981319).

## Practice guides actually examined

OPM's full public Structured Interviews guide connects job analysis, capability selection, question and rating design, probe rules, piloting, and documentation. Its guidance on comparable probe opportunities motivates documenting permitted neutral probes before use. It is a historical federal guide; do not use it as a current global legal standard. [Guide](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews/guide.pdf).

Canada's detailed public guide includes before/during/after administration, independent ratings, common assessment errors, and applicant reactions. Its page is under review. Use it for process design and refresh local policy separately. [Guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-service-commission/services/public-service-hiring-guides/appointment-processes-how-conduct-interviews.html).

## How these sources affect the skill

| Implementation | Grounding | Evidence to test locally |
|---|---|---|
| Answer inventory then rehearsal | Public practitioner workflows | Candidate can retrieve a relevant real event and explain it independently |
| Diagnosis before rewriting | Authored synthesis from coaching and task analysis | Intervention addresses the observed issue rather than fabricating content |
| Evidence-linked critique and disagreement | Conversate's qualitative findings; authored evidence boundaries | Agent revises a mistaken critique but does not agree with unsupported claims |
| Bounded retry and transfer | Conversate's reported revision difficulty; learning-oriented design hypothesis | New question elicits the target behavior without full model support |
| Structured probes and independent ratings | OPM and Canada process guidance | Questions do not leak the answer; missing evidence stays distinct from low capability |
| Profession-specific task criteria | Employer/professional process sources and occupational tasks | Agent assesses the relevant work rather than generic fluency |
| Responsibility-specific depth | Advertised role scope and published grade frameworks | Apprentice, senior IC, and manager are not judged against the same authority |

The authored interventions have not been isolated experimentally. Full-text access failures remain explicit; no inaccessible study is presented as a reviewed complete experiment. Further bibliographic and specialist review can improve the skill without blocking useful, bounded coaching now.

## Outcome ladder

Distinguish: user satisfaction; clearer practice answers; improved unassisted performance on different questions; actual interview ratings; employment offers; later job performance. Evidence at one level does not establish the next. The local evaluations target observable response quality and instruction-following, not hiring outcomes or causal effects.
