### Decision Path (if conflicting evidence exists)

When health signals conflict (e.g., adoption is strong but support-sentiment
is declining), the record must:

1. **Surface the conflict** — name both signals explicitly.
2. **Propose an explanation** — what could explain the disagreement?
3. **Define an investigation path** — what additional evidence would resolve
   the conflict?
4. **Assign a decision-maker** — who decides the next action?
5. **Set a review date** — when will the conflict be re-examined?

This ensures the conflict is not buried in an average or a single "green /
yellow / red" indicator.
