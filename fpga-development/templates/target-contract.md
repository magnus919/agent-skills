## Completion instructions

Use `UNKNOWN` for absent evidence and name the artifact that resolves it. A board photo may support label identity but not pin mapping. A USB VID/PID may support the bridge family but not FPGA configuration mode. The reviewer should reject the contract when a missing value could cause electrical damage, an invalid constraint, or an unrecoverable programming action.

## Example decision

`HOLD`: oscillator frequency is printed as 12 MHz but no schematic or datasheet confirms tolerance or pin. Request the board schematic and device clock input limits before creating timing constraints.
