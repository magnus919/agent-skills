# Research brief and log

## Scope set before synthesis

**Question:** Can one reusable, source-grounded solarpunk skill serve creative work and
practical ecological futures across places without reducing the movement to a fixed visual
style, one political program, or unsupported claims of feasibility?

**Depth and method:** broad multi-angle foundation, not a systematic review or exhaustive
global survey. Research conducted 2026-10-28. Durable destination: this skill's tracked
references, templates and evals. Inclusion: primary movement accounts, editorial/publisher
records, public technical/institutional guidance, practitioner cases and relevant research.
Older foundational texts are retained; practical details require current local verification.
English-language retrieval predominates, a significant limitation. Exclude unattributed
image lists, shopping promotion, unsupported universal claims, and private anecdotes.

## Findings and confidence

1. **High for documented breadth:** ReDes, Flynn and Springett connect cultural imagination
   with practical agency. They share intellectual lineages, so are not independent proof
   of impact. Their differences support an open router rather than a single definition.
2. **High for editorial divergence, limited generality:** the retrieved magazine call
   accommodates horror and accessible futures; publisher metadata adds more-than-human and
   Asia-Pacific reading paths. Neither establishes the content or quality of unread stories.
3. **Moderate for transferable practice:** repair, training, shared generation, agroecology,
   public-space reclamation and vessel electrification offer concrete mechanisms. Provider
   accounts establish activities/descriptions more strongly than independent outcomes.
4. **High for existence of a tactical spectrum:** first-person Park(ing) Day history and
   the Tactical Urbanism landing page document guerrilla origins and sanctioned/unsanctioned
   practices. Permission-seeking must not be imposed as a cultural definition of solarpunk.
5. **Bounded empirical finding:** seed-ball extension guidance describes promise; a 2026
   field-study abstract reports an emergence benefit without improved two-year establishment.
   Different evidence classes and settings do not justify universal efficacy claims.

## Extraction, synthesis and preservation

The source ledger contains an individual record for each retained source, including reading
leads, supported finding, access/evidence limits and destination. Domain guides supply
original workflows and examples derived from those findings; templates and rubrics are
**authored design proposals**, not empirically validated interventions. Citations support
specified claims, not every adjacent recommendation. The barge is explicitly a fictional
composite, with no private attribution or claimed identification.

**Evidence table:** cultural breadth S01–S05; participation/institutions S06/S19–S22;
physical systems S07–S14/S17–S18/S23–S26; public cases S10/S15–S16/S27–S31;
seed-ball promise/limits S33–S35. Claims are preserved in the corresponding domain guide
and ledger entry. No essential evidence is intended to exist only in chat.

## Gaps, confidence and refresh

This is an English-dominant, non-exhaustive foundation. It is not a globally representative
survey, an independently evaluated catalog of successful projects, or a systematic seed-ball
review. Costs, local species, safety standards, law, programs, availability and present
project status need fresh local research. Primary accounts do not establish independent
causal outcomes. Limited/blocked access is disclosed above and in each source record.

Future deepening should prioritize non-English movement voices, locally led accounts,
measured retrofit/participation results and longitudinal maintenance/failure evidence.
The skill's performance also remains a separate question: structural checks and authored
eval cases cannot establish runtime quality, calibrated accuracy or user benefit.

**Preservation check:** retained sources have records; material findings and tensions have
routed guides; rejected/blocked leads are recorded; source URLs, date and limits are retained.
