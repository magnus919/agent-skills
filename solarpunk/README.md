# Solarpunk — imagine better futures and start building them

## Why Install This Skill

Solarpunk can be a beautiful picture, a story worth inhabiting, or a better way to live
right now. This skill helps an agent move between those possibilities: design a vivid
scene, write a hopeful story with real conflict, improve a rented home, or plan a
community project around the people who will use and maintain it.

It brings together ecological design, adaptive reuse, everyday resourcefulness, and
collective imagination. Guidance adapts to climates, cultures, budgets, and abilities
around the world. Its politics lean toward ecological care and shared agency while
remaining open to your own framing. Research references distinguish inspiring ideas
from demonstrated results.

## What You Get

| Contents | Provides |
|---|---|
| `SKILL.md` | A workflow connecting creative requests and practical action |
| `references/` | Thirteen focused guides covering art, fiction, daily life, settlements, living systems, governance, reuse, tactical action, education, evidence, and research provenance |

## Quick Start

Ask your agent:

- “Design a solarpunk winter neighborhood in a cold climate.”
- “Write a story about a cooperative repair shop facing a difficult decision.”
- “I rent a small flat. Help me try solarpunk living without buying new gadgets.”
- “Explore turning an old industrial barge into a planted, solar-electric home.”

Expect specific creative work or a practical pathway with costs to investigate,
maintenance responsibilities, and a first step that fits your circumstances.

## Triggers

- Solarpunk art, image prompts, fashion, interiors, stories, or games
- Hopeful ecological futures and how people could reach them
- Solarpunk approaches to reuse, housing, food, energy, and community life
- Parklets, guerrilla gardening and direct action within solarpunk
- Critiques of greenwashed futures or inaccessible visions of sustainability

## Requirements

No packages or API keys are required to read and use the skill. Actual image/video
generation needs a suitable tool. Current local recommendations need web access;
construction and specialist installations need locally qualified professionals.
