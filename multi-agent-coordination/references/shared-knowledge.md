# Correct shared practices without reintroducing them

A mistaken convention can propagate innocently through summaries and inherited
notes. A malicious instruction can propagate through similar paths. Keep these
diagnoses separate: infection is a useful analogy, not proof of an attacker or a
biological model. [Prompt Infection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.07283v1) demonstrates
agent-to-agent injection in its tested setups and studies combined defenses;
source tagging alone is not a general guarantee.

## Prevention

Keep authoritative policies, accepted decisions, observations, hypotheses and
personal preferences distinct. Give shared claims stable IDs, source version,
scope, owner, evidence, review status and invalidation triggers. Preserve a
source-to-derived-artifact graph where the decision warrants it. Repetition by
several agents does not create independent corroboration. A signed message or
familiar colleague is still capable of transmitting false or untrusted content.

Promote candidate lessons through harness-engineering's lesson-to-PR process.
Source correction must not silently broaden authority or edit protected controls.
Retrieve by reference rather than copying every rule into every prompt. Bound
private evidence access/retention and use sanitized fictional fixtures in public
evals. Do not persist secrets or raw hidden reasoning for tracing.

## Containment procedure

1. Preserve a minimized incident record and identify the earliest known source,
   source revision and conflicting authoritative evidence. Distinguish known bad,
   suspected and merely exposed artifacts.
2. Stop affected promotion/retrieval paths within existing authority. Quarantine
   candidate lessons; do not delete unrelated work or indiscriminately reset a fleet.
3. Traverse known derivation and consumption edges across notes, caches, indexes,
   role recaps, queued assignments and active sessions. Record unknown coverage;
   missing telemetry prevents an eradication claim.
4. Publish a versioned correction through the appropriate owner with a
   supersession/tombstone relation and a clear reason. Repair current source,
   derived summaries and retrieval indexes. Preserve later valid edits.
5. Notify affected consumers through authorized channels. Distinguish delivered,
   acknowledged, adopted and behaviorally verified. An acknowledgement alone does
   not clear a cached context or an already queued operation.
6. Reconcile effects already taken; restart/checkpoint affected workers if the
   host requires it. Challenge a fresh session and a late stale message to ensure
   the old practice does not return through backups or extraction jobs.
7. Observe recurrence over declared exposure and time. No incident with no
   exposure is inconclusive; document gaps and residual consumers.

Use the knowledge-correction template. The helper's `exposure` command traces
only supplied directed edges. It does not find hidden copies, judge truth or
modify memory. Route implementation to the owning harness/data system and
production incident handling to agent-production-operations.
