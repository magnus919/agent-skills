# Package a team's context and preserve useful lessons

Use when replicating a competent team across hosts or projects, publishing a team
blueprint, or turning experience into shared organizational memory. Source records
S28–S39 ground the design; no bundle was installed or launched in this research.

## Blueprint, package and qualified team are different artifacts

A blueprint declares roles, topology and contracts. A package carries that
blueprint plus declared skills, context, plugins and supporting resources. A
qualified team has actually exercised its outcome and boundaries on the intended
host/project configuration. Do not label one as the other.

Use templates/team-package.md to record exact source/configuration IDs, content
manifest, runtime/model/plugin dependencies, startup and compaction maps,
capability/ownership routes, communication/acceptance contracts, budgets, authority
ceilings, protected controls, target-conflict handling and rollback. Document
resources resolved from the installing host separately from packaged bytes.
Changing a provider, harness, context pack or host policy may invalidate evidence
without changing an archive's digest.

Before importing, inspect untrusted instructions, scripts, hooks, symlinks/path
escapes, dependencies, network/side-effect surfaces and credential references.
An integrity digest detects covered content changes; it is not provenance
verification, a security audit, authority or a result receipt. Check precisely
which bytes/metadata the digest covers. Do not package credentials or live private
session state by default, and never infer recipient grants from producer settings.

Preserve existing repository instructions and unrelated work; bind installation
and launch to their separately authorized target and scope. A creator's
permission-bypass posture is an opinionated deployment choice, not a portable
best practice or permission for our environment. A repeatable launch still needs
current resource reservations, effective grants and protected supervision.

## Qualify the specific combination

Exercise one accepted job plus onboarding, compaction, missing route, absent
specialist, canceled/late worker and integrated acceptance on the actual intended
configuration. Record host/harness/model versions, source/configuration/package
identities and observed effects. Component harness checks do not establish a team
run, and an old successful configuration does not establish the new package.
Unsupported or untested combinations stay explicit. Min-version checks and
static manifests do not prove behavioral compatibility.

The OpenRig Workshop registry explicitly distinguishes prior harness checks from
an end-to-end run of its listed team configuration. Its published package also
identifies host-resolved resources and broad permission defaults. These are useful
examples of evidence/authority disclosures, not endorsements of those defaults.
Follow the named tool's current documentation for operations; this reference owns
the portable qualification questions.

## Distinguish support, authority and age

Shared notes need source, scope, evidence, owner, review status and invalidation
rules. Raw observations, candidate lessons and accepted project guidance may use
project-defined names; no universal maturity ladder is required. A new verified
finding can supersede an old convention. An old note does not become reliable
through repetition, age or a familiar author's name. Evidence support, policy
acceptance, freshness and permission remain separate dimensions.

Accepted guidance needs supporting observations and a path to contradictions,
review and supersession. Preserve the history rather than silently overwriting
why a decision changed. PROV supplies a provenance vocabulary, not a truth score.
Use harness-engineering's lesson-to-PR process for promotion and this skill's
shared-knowledge procedure for containment/consumer correction.

## Store a lesson with its boundary, not only its slogan

A short narrative can connect situation → choice → consequence → observed outcome
→ reusable principle. Use templates/lesson-story.md. Keep the original evidence,
scope, counterexample and review owner attached. A fictional parable can aid
explanation but cannot substitute for an incident receipt or prove the proposed
fix works. Avoid inventing a causal chain merely because it makes a good story.

A memorable cue should retrieve the scoped lesson, not authorize an action or
apply a blanket prohibition. For example, a scope-creep story should not suppress
an explicitly approved expansion or a necessary safety dependency. Test transfer
on a different task and on a legitimate near miss before promoting it as shared
guidance. Review inherited lore at handover; named addresses make it discoverable,
not automatically trustworthy. A story's popularity is not evidence of correctness.
