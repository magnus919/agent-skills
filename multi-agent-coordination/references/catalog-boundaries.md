# Catalog ownership and handoffs

This skill owns cross-agent methodology. It composes specialist procedures;
it does not move all orchestration-related material out of other skills.

| Owner | Supply | Receive |
|---|---|---|
| [harness-engineering](../../harness-engineering/SKILL.md) | Cross-agent scope/budget/cancel/context/ownership obligations | Actual runtime enforcement, persistence and lifecycle evidence; unsupported host features |
| [promise-theory](../../promise-theory/SKILL.md) | Agents, offers, reliance and observed breaches | Promise/acceptance model, assessments and renegotiation responsibilities |
| [agent-evals-and-observability](../../agent-evals-and-observability/SKILL.md) | Team hypotheses, task/trajectory contracts and failure fixtures | Comparable eval design, outcome uncertainty and telemetry/grader limitations |
| [agent-production-operations](../../agent-production-operations/SKILL.md) | Evaluated topology, authority/containment obligations and recovery limits | Rollout/control decisions, observed incidents and operational evidence |
| [ai-governance](../../ai-governance/SKILL.md) | Decision map, authority evidence, exceptions and human workload | Accountable grants/ceilings and reserved decisions |
| [langgraph](../../langgraph/SKILL.md), [autogen](../../autogen/SKILL.md), [crewai](../../crewai/SKILL.md), [pydanticai](../../pydanticai/SKILL.md) | Chosen topology, state/message and acceptance contracts | Framework implementation plus actual execution evidence |
| [agent-council](../../agent-council/SKILL.md) | Bounded debate question, evidence and unresolved alternatives | Decision landscape; consensus is not correctness or permission |
| [operational-design](../../operational-design/SKILL.md), [ai-operating-economics](../../ai-operating-economics/SKILL.md) | Flow, human work, accepted outcomes and full cost | Business process/capacity and value decisions |
| [research-methodology](../../research-methodology/SKILL.md) | Tool or research claim and adoption decision | Primary-source extraction, contradictions and preserved evidence |
| [systematic-debugging](../../systematic-debugging/SKILL.md), [incident-learning](../../incident-learning/SKILL.md) | First divergence, bounded trace and observed effects | Supported cause or unresolved hypothesis; owned corrective actions |

For recurring production use harness-engineering's existing factory charter,
steering and supervision procedures. This skill supplies the team-level design
and failure analysis; the existing runtime/factory record remains authoritative.
Do not maintain a second task truth merely because another template is available.

Existing operational tool skills for HERDR, Block/Buzz and OpenRig are not
present in this catalog at this research revision. Link their official manuals
as external evidence rather than inventing dead skill routes. Future named-tool
skills should route up here for design, while retaining their own commands.
