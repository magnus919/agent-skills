## Transition Card

1. Observe and record the approved trigger evidence.
2. Follow the named authority and local transition procedure.
3. Announce or coordinate the new tier as the approved procedure requires.
4. Obtain the required acknowledgment from the other endpoint.
5. Record time, decision-maker, current tier, and unresolved uncertainty.
6. If alignment fails, use the approved fallback or handoff; do not improvise identifiers or authority.

## Stop And Escalate

- Card status is not `APPROVED`, or an operational field is unresolved: do not distribute it as an operating aid.
- Immediate danger: use emergency services and authorized incident procedures.
- Missing authority or procedure: stop and contact the named decision-maker.
- No approved path remains: follow the approved no-communications or handoff procedure.
- Unsafe, destructive, or out-of-scope action: stop and preserve the evidence.
