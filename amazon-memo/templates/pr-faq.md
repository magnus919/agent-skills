## Customer FAQ

- Who is this for, and who is it not for?
- What task changes, and what remains the customer's responsibility?
- How would use, access, price, data handling, and support work?
- What limits or failure cases should a customer understand?

## Internal FAQ

- What evidence supports the need? What is observed versus assumed, and what contradicts it?
- Why this approach over the status quo and other credible solutions?
- What must be technically and operationally true? Which dependencies are unproven?
- What are the economics, resource commitments, and displaced priorities?
- What risks, trust concerns, or failure modes could outweigh the benefit?
- What smallest test could disprove the idea? What evidence would justify further investment?
- Who owns the next decision, when is it due, and what decision is requested now?

Expand questions that expose real uncertainty; omit irrelevant ones. The FAQ is not
a promotional appendix. Keep launch aspirations separate from verified capabilities.
