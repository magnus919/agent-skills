# amazon-memo

Write narrative memos that help people reason and decide.

## Why Install This Skill

A six-page target can produce six pages of padding. A polished proposal can hide
the very uncertainty its readers need to discuss. This skill helps your agent
choose a useful form, explain the tradeoffs, and write clear prose supported by evidence.

It covers Amazon-style decision narratives and PR/FAQs, plus shorter briefs and
adaptations for other written cultures. It separates documented practice from
recommendations and includes practical revision tests for mechanical AI prose.
All templates and examples are original, generic, and suitable for public use.

## What You Get

| Contents | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `SKILL.md` | Form selection, drafting, revision, and completion workflow |
| `references/` | Source boundaries, length planning, review practices, readability tests, synthetic examples |
| `templates/` | Decision narrative, PR/FAQ, and short decision brief |
| `evals/evals.json` | Representative quality cases, including evidence and adaptation pitfalls |

## Quick Start

Ask your agent: “Use amazon-memo to turn these notes into a one-page decision
brief for a 20-minute team review. Explain the alternatives and preserve the unknowns.”

Or: “Review this PR/FAQ for unsupported claims and rewrite the AI-heavy prose
without losing technical detail.” No setup commands are required.

## Triggers

- Amazon-style narratives, six-pagers, working-backwards PR/FAQs
- Choosing between a decision memo, product proposal, and short brief
- Silent-reading meetings or durable async memo review
- Revising inflated or mechanical prose in a decision document

## Requirements

No runtime dependencies, accounts, or API keys. Actual page-count verification
requires a document renderer. This skill drafts and reviews; it does not send or publish.
