the implementing agent relied on that supplied verification, not a separate fetch.

## Synthesis and exclusions

The short-brief scaffold, 20-minute agenda example, readability tests, and routing
defaults are this skill's recommendations. They combine decision-writing judgment
with the source mechanisms; they are not sourced corporate templates. The worked
examples are invented and establish no factual claim about customer behavior.

Secondary search results were excluded from corporate-practice claims in favor of
company sources and firsthand accounts. No leaked internal memo, private company
material, personal interview notes, or proprietary example was used. Exact corporate
templates that could not be fetched were not reconstructed from guesswork.

No authenticated crawler was used. The unavailable crawler route was excluded and
was not retried; public sources and supplied research were sufficient for scope.

Open questions: actual receiving-team layout and governance must be supplied or
explicitly assumed for each use. Company practices can evolve; recheck the relevant
primary source when current policy, rather than a described historical mechanism,
is material. This skill does not claim a universal or exhaustive Amazon memo taxonomy.
