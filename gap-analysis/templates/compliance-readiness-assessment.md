## Status meanings

- **Met and evidenced:** the condition and evidence match the scope.
- **Implemented, unproven:** a control exists but its operation or boundary has not been demonstrated.
- **Partial:** only part of the condition is met.
- **Not met:** evidence shows the condition is absent.
- **Not applicable:** documented rationale and approving authority required.
- **Unknown:** evidence unavailable or contradictory; do not treat as met.
